# Payroll (Mexico + USA)

## Pay run lifecycle
draft → calculating → calculated → approved → paid (also voided). Each transition fires webhooks (payroll.run.calculated/approved/paid). When posting to the GL: debit salaries + employer contributions; credit banks + tax/withholding liabilities.

## Employees
- MX data: RFC, CURP, NSS, integrated daily salary. USA: SSN, W-4 (2020+), work_state/residence_state (multi-state supported).
- Versioned compensation (history); termination via POST /employees/:id/terminate.

## Mexico
- ISR (SAT brackets), IMSS (employee-employer), INFONAVIT. Caps in UMA.
- Payroll CFDI 1.2: POST /paychecks/:id/cfdi-nomina (stamps via multi-PAC). ONLY for a paycheck whose run is approved or paid (#601): for a draft, calculating, calculated or voided run it answers 409 before the PAC is called and writes nothing, so the run can still be corrected and calculated again. The next step for a calculated run is `mnemosine pay-run approve <run> --dry-run`, never the stamp.
- SUA (POST /sua), severance/settlement (POST /finiquito — year-end bonus, vacation premium; the payroll CFDI says TipoNomina E only for a bonus, final or off-cycle RUN, never because of the finiquito endpoint).
  The finiquito computes on the SALARIO DIARIO, never on the SBC (the SBC is the integrated salary and is for IMSS contributions only); it reads `dias_aguinaldo` and `prima_vacacional_pct` from the policy panel, applies the LFT art. 76 vacation table (12 days year 1, +2/year to 20 at year 5, then +2 every five years), prorates the aguinaldo from the hire date when the employee joined mid-year (art. 87), and returns every amount as a STRING with four decimals plus a `basis` block saying which seniority, table row and daily wage produced it. Its `basis` also reports the last ordinary monthly salary (USMO: the daily wage × `usmo_days_per_month`, 30 by default) and `usmo_isr_subsidy` (`gross_of_tariff` by default), the two panel readings of the rate of LISR art. 96 (the paragraph on separation payments). The result's `separation_isr` applies the exemption of LISR art. 93 fr. XIII (90 UMA per year of service, a fraction of more than six months counting as a year) to the seniority premium ALONE; pending salary, aguinaldo and vacation premium keep the ordinary route on the payroll run. The taxable part is withheld by LISR art. 96: at the ISR of the USMO by the MONTHLY tariff divided by the USMO (`usmo_isr_subsidy` decides gross or net of the subsidy), or by the monthly tariff on the taxable part when the payments compared with the USMO are below it (`separation_isr_usmo_comparison`: `total_payments` by default, or `taxable_payments`). `separation_isr.withholding` is that ISR; `total` stays gross and no net is given, since the ordinary items' ISR is not in it. Without the seeded 90 UMA row, `separation_isr` says `not_calculated` and names the row, never a figure.
  The SENIORITY PREMIUM (LFT art. 162, 12 days per year) is capped at twice the minimum wage of the ZONE of the place of work (art. 486): the employee's `minimum_wage_zone` when set, else the entity's (`general` or `northern_border`, set with `mnemosine entity create|edit --minimum-wage-zone`). In 2026 that is 2 × 315.04 or 2 × 440.87, so fifteen years at 1 000 a day are 113 414.40 or 158 713.20. An entity that never declared its zone is computed with the general minimum and `seniority_premium_minimum_wage.declared_by` is null: say so, and give the `entity edit` command (`mnemosine pending` shows the same warning). A zone whose minimum is not loaded for the date is NOT replaced by the general one: the premium says SIN CALCULAR and names the days owed.
- IMSS IDSE: /imss-idse/batch produces the fixed-width .txt. There is NO submit: /imss-idse/submit answers 501. A human uploads the file at idse.imss.gob.mx with the patron FIEL and keeps the acuse.

## USA
- FIT (Pub 15-T), FICA (SS with annual cap + Medicare + additional 0.9%), FUTA ($7k), SUTA PER STATE (each state has its own base; SUTA YTD is tracked by work_state).
- Forms: W-2 (/w2; box 1 = FIT base without 401k, box 3 = FICA base with 401k capped at the SS wage base), W-3 (/w3), EFW2 (/efw2), 941 (/form-941), 940 (/form-940).
- Direct deposit: NACHA (/nacha) produces the file; the human delivers it to the bank. ONLY for an approved or paid run (#601): for any other it answers 409, writes no batch and reads no account.
- mnemosine does NOT transmit to any tax authority. /irs-efile/:filing_id, its status endpoint and /ssa-bso/submit all answer 501. The forms above are produced here; filing them is a HUMAN act — an authorized e-file provider or mail for the IRS, the BSO portal for the EFW2 — and the confirmation number is recorded on the filing afterwards.
- Benefits: 401k/HSA/FSA/Sec-125 plans (GET /benefit-plans, elections per employee).

## Self-service (authenticated employee)
GET /me/paychecks, GET /me/w2/:tax_year.

## What YOU do
- NEVER tell a human that mnemosine has filed, transmitted or submitted anything to the IRS, the SSA or IMSS. It cannot, and the endpoints that once implied otherwise now refuse.
- Payroll runs from the TERMINAL, and a human runs it: `mnemosine employee create|show|list`, `mnemosine pay-schedule create|list` and `mnemosine pay-period create|list` (the calendar a run needs: `pay-period create --schedule <id> --count <n>` writes the next periods after the last one and never writes one twice), `mnemosine pay-run create|calculate|approve|post` (post leaves the run's entry as a draft for `mnemosine review`; `--post` posts it), `mnemosine payslip list --run <id>|show <id>` and `mnemosine imss sua export --period YYYY-MM -o <file>`. None of them is a tool of yours: you query payroll journal entries in the ledger (search_journal_entries / get_general_ledger), explain calculations and rules, and give the human the ONE next command with its exact flags — or the /v1/payroll/... endpoint for what has no command yet (CFDI stamping, finiquito, IDSE, the USA forms). NEVER estimate taxes "by eye": if asked for an exact calculation, tell them to calculate the run and read the paycheck with `payslip show`.
- A RUN IS CALCULATED WHOLE OR NOT AT ALL, AND AGAIN UNTIL IT IS APPROVED (#601): `pay-run calculate` writes every paycheck of the run in one transaction; if one employee fails, no paycheck is written and the run keeps the status, the totals and the paychecks it had. A draft or calculated run can be calculated again: its paychecks are REPLACED by those of the inputs file (an employee left out of the file loses the paycheck) and its totals are their sum. So a calculated run with a wrong figure is corrected by calculating it again with the WHOLE corrected file, never by creating a second run of the period. A PERIOD HOLDS ONE REGULAR RUN (#601): `pay-run create` refuses a second run of type `regular` for a period that has one in any status but voided, and names the one it has; a payment apart from the regular one (aguinaldo, PTU, a final settlement, an off-cycle payment) still gets its own run with `--type bonus|final|off_cycle`. Runs created before the rule are not touched. An approved or paid run is refused, and so is a run one of whose paychecks already has a payroll CFDI or is in a deposit batch.
- A YEARLY VALUE NOT LOADED BY THE PAY DATE IS NAMED, NOT SILENT (#953): the minimum wages take a new value on 1 January and the UMA on 1 February. When a Mexican run is paid on or after that day and the value in force is still one that began before it, `pay-run calculate` warns, per parameter, which value it used, since when it rules and from when the new one was due (under `--json`, the row's `warnings`; in the table, a line on stderr). The run is still calculated with that value and no amount changes: tell the human to load the new value from its DOF publication and calculate the run again, never to treat the old figure as the new one. A value whose row ended and has no successor is not a warning: the run fails naming it.
- The SUA file: `imss sua export` sums the approved paychecks of the month and checks employer IMSS and INFONAVIT against `employer_tax_liabilities`; if they differ it refuses the file (exit 4), says both figures and records nothing; do not suggest a way around the check, hand the two figures to a human. `--dry-run` builds and checks without writing or recording. A delivered file is recorded as ONE DRAFT filing per entity and month (a re-export refreshes it); nothing reaches the IMSS until a human loads it into the SUA and pays. Without `-o` the file is refused on an interactive terminal: it carries the NSS, RFC and CURP of the whole roll.

## Employer taxes and the employment subsidy (F08a)
- Every tax component of a paycheck is now stored per row in `paycheck_taxes` (base, rate, EE/ER side, credit flag). Forms that used to report zeros read this. The employer side accrues to `employer_tax_liabilities` when the pay run is approved, one row per tax and — for the ISN — one row per state. IMSS and INFONAVIT for a period that straddles two months are split by contribution days into one row per month per tax (a week from February 25th to March 3rd is 4/7 February, 3/7 March), and the SUA file uses the same split, so each month ties out. If a share lands in a month whose SUA was already generated or whose contributions were already deposited, the approval says so (`share_in_already_settled_month`): that month needs a complementary filing. Runs approved before the split keep a whole-period row until a human runs `npm run backfill:straddling-liabilities -- --tenant <id>` (census by default, `--apply` to write).
- SUBSIDIO AL EMPLEO LARGER THAN THE ISR OF THE PERIOD (#600): what happens to the difference is the law of the PAYMENT date, a dated row of `legal_parameters` (`employment_subsidy.excess_over_isr_paid_share`), not a policy of the firm. Paid from 1 May 2024 (decree DOF 01-05-2024): the ISR withheld is zero, NOTHING is handed to the worker and nothing is carried forward; net pay does not grow, `paychecks.subsidio_entregado_efectivo` is 0 and the payroll entry books nothing for the excess, neither a receivable nor an expense. `paychecks.subsidio_empleo` still holds the whole subsidy caused, and `calculation_details` names the excess not handed over and the row applied. The payroll CFDI declares it as `OtrosPagos` `TipoOtroPago="002"` with `Importe="0.00"`, `SubsidioCausado` equal to the whole subsidy caused and `TotalOtrosPagos="0.00"`. Paid until 30 April 2024: the employer HANDS THE DIFFERENCE TO THE WORKER IN CASH; it lands in `paychecks.subsidio_entregado_efectivo`, raises net pay, is declared in the same CFDI node with `Importe` equal to that cash, and is posted against the account the policy `subsidio_al_empleo_entregado_registro` names. That policy governs only cash that was in fact handed over. Never tell a worker paid from May 2024 that the excess is owed in cash. A paycheck already calculated keeps its figures until it is calculated again, and one already paid with the cash is not corrected by the system: hand it to a human. When the ISR is at or above the subsidy the same node is written with `Importe="0.00"` and the subsidy caused, and the `Deduccion` 002 carries only the ISR retained after it (#92).
- HOW MUCH SUBSIDY (#298): since the decree of 1 May 2024 it is NOT a bracket table. It is a percentage of the MONTHLY UMA, read from `legal_parameters` on the PAYMENT date, for workers whose monthly income does not exceed the cap (11 492.66 in 2026). In 2026 it is 15.59 % of the 2025 UMA in January and 15.02 % of the 2026 UMA from February; a week gets monthly × 7 / 30.4 and a quincena monthly × 15 / 30.4. The rounding is the policy `subsidio_al_empleo_redondeo`: `producto_al_centavo` (default) gives 535.65 / 264.30 / 123.34 from February and 536.21 / 264.58 / 123.47 in January; `diario_al_centavo` rounds the daily amount first and only changes January (quincena 264.60). A payment date without the law loaded fails instead of paying zero. Catorcena, semimonthly and annual periods are refused, as for ISR.
- ONE SUBSIDY PER PAY PERIOD (#430): when the worker already has a paycheck in another calculated, approved or paid run of the same pay period (an aguinaldo paid in its own run, for example), the policy `employment_subsidy_separate_run` decides. `recompute_on_combined_income` (default) computes the subsidy once on the ISR base of all those paychecks plus this one and credits only the difference, never below zero; `none_on_separate_paycheck` gives none to the SEPARATE paycheck (a run other than `regular`, or one that pays only aguinaldo) and lets the regular one keep its own, whichever was calculated first. A negative difference (combined income over the cap) is not recovered: that is an open question to the owner on #430. The later paycheck's `subsidio_empleo` is that difference, so its net pay, CFDI and entry follow it.
- IMSS AND INFONAVIT ONCE PER PAY PERIOD (#601): the quotas are owed for the contribution days of the period on the SBC (LSS arts. 27 to 29 and 39; Ley del INFONAVIT art. 29), never for a payment, so a period's days contribute ONCE whatever the number of runs. When the worker already has a paycheck in another calculated, approved or paid run of the same pay period, the new paycheck charges only what those have not: the worker's and the employer's IMSS quota, the employer's 5 % INFONAVIT and the worker's INFONAVIT credit discount are each the period's figure less what was already charged, never below zero, and `taxable_wages_imss` is the part of the base nobody contributed on. So an aguinaldo paid in its own run after the fortnight carries 0.00 in all four and is paid less its ISR only; its `paycheck_taxes` rows say «quota of the period X; its other paychecks already charged Y», and `calculation_details.contributions_charged_by_other_paychecks_of_period` holds the figures. The paycheck calculated FIRST carries the days, whatever its run type: if the aguinaldo run is calculated before the fortnight, the aguinaldo paycheck carries the quotas and the fortnight none; the period's total is the same. This is the law, not a policy of the panel. The SUA file counts a period's days once per worker, and an approval does not raise `imss_patronal_en_cero` for such a paycheck. Paychecks calculated before this rule keep their figures until their run is calculated again; an approved or paid run that charged a period twice is not corrected by the system: hand it to a human. The ISR of a payment made apart is the next rule.
- THE ISR OF A PAYMENT MADE APART (#601): when the worker already has a paycheck in another calculated, approved or paid run of the same pay period, or the paycheck is itself separate (a run other than `regular`, or one that pays only aguinaldo), the policy `separate_payment_isr` decides its ISR. `period_accumulated_base` (default) applies the period's tariff to the ISR base of those paychecks plus this one and withholds that less the ISR they already caused, never below zero; so an aguinaldo paid apart after the fortnight withholds at the marginal rate of the period, and the period's ISR is the same whichever was calculated first. `own_paycheck_base` taxes each paycheck on its own base alone, as the engine did before. `regulation_optional_procedure` (RLISR art. 174) is refused by key for the separate paycheck: the text of that procedure is not in the corpus, so it is never computed from memory; tell the human to answer another option or to have the text added. The paycheck records the treatment and its figures in `calculation_details.separate_payment_isr`. A regular paycheck alone in its period does not read the key and keeps its figure. Paychecks already calculated are not corrected.
- WHO ONLY EARNS THE MINIMUM WAGE SUFFERS NO ISR WITHHOLDING (#1076; LISR art. 96, first paragraph; source: Cámara de Diputados, «Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta», text with its last reform DOF 01-04-2024, https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LISR.pdf, read 2026-11-27): «Quienes hagan pagos por los conceptos a que se refiere este Capítulo están obligados a efectuar retenciones y enteros mensuales que tendrán el carácter de pagos provisionales a cuenta del impuesto anual. No se efectuará retención a las personas que en el mes únicamente perciban un salario mínimo general correspondiente al área geográfica del contribuyente.» How the paycheck reads it. WHO: the worker whose contractual daily wage (`annual_salary` / 365) is at most the daily minimum of their zone on the payment date (the employee's `minimum_wage_zone`, else the entity's, else `general`), the same test the overtime exemption uses; with no `annual_salary` loaded the worker cannot be told to earn the minimum and the tariff applies as before, so tell the human to load it. ONLY: the ISR base of the worker's paychecks of the pay period (exempt earnings do not count) is at most the minimum wage of the period, the daily minimum times its days: the calendar days of the period, and never fewer than the days of the tariff applied (7 a week, 15 a fortnight, 30.4 a month), so 16 to 28 February measures 15 days and 16 to 31 July measures 16. In 2026 a fortnight of 4 725.60 in the general zone (6 613.05 at the border) withholds 0.00 and 4 725.61 withholds by the tariff. Then `paychecks.isr_withheld` is 0, the ISR row of `paycheck_taxes` stays with amount 0 and a note that names the article and the tariff figure not withheld, `calculation_details.minimum_wage_isr_exemption` records zone, days, minimum, base and treatment, and `payslip show` says why. The subsidy is still caused (`subsidio_empleo`) but nothing is credited, since nothing is withheld, and under the rule in force since 1 May 2024 nothing is handed over (`calculation_details.employment_subsidy_excess_not_paid` holds it). THAT WORKER'S CFDI carries no `Deduccion` 002 and no `TotalImpuestosRetenidos`, and `OtrosPagos` 002 with `Importe="0.00"` and `SubsidioCausado` equal to the subsidy caused (264.30 in a fortnight of 2026). The sources, read 2026-11-27. (a) Decree that grants the employment subsidy (DOF 01-05-2024, https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle.php?codigo=5725287&fecha=01/05/2024), Artículo Segundo, first paragraph as last reformed (DOF 31-12-2025, https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle.php?codigo=5777649&fecha=31/12/2025): «Se otorga un subsidio para el empleo mensual a las personas trabajadoras a que hace referencia el Artículo Primero de este decreto, cuyos ingresos mensuales que sirvan de base para calcular el impuesto sobre la renta correspondiente al mes de calendario de que se trate, no excedan de $11,492.66 [...] Dicho subsidio para el empleo se aplicará contra el impuesto sobre la renta correspondiente al mes de calendario de que se trate y que resulte a cargo de las referidas personas trabajadoras, en términos del artículo 96 de la Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta.»; its second paragraph (text of 2024): «En los casos en que el impuesto a cargo del trabajador sea menor que el subsidio para el empleo mensual obtenido conforme a este artículo, la diferencia no podrá aplicarse contra el impuesto que resulte a su cargo posteriormente, ni se entregará cantidad alguna por concepto del subsidio para el empleo establecido en este artículo.»; Artículo Tercero, fr. I, on the annual computation: «El impuesto a cargo del trabajador se disminuirá con la suma de las cantidades que por concepto de subsidio para el empleo mensual le correspondió al trabajador.» (b) SAT, «Guía de llenado del comprobante del recibo de pago de nómina y su complemento» (CFDI 4.0, complement 1.2, last change 16 Jan 2026, http://omawww.sat.gob.mx/tramitesyservicios/Paginas/documentos/Guia_llenado_Nomina.pdf): field SubsidioCausado, «Se debe registrar el subsidio causado conforme a la normatividad vigente. El valor de este dato debe ser mayor o igual que el valor del campo “Importe” de la Información de OtroPago.»; Apéndice 6, what the SAT takes for the prefilled annual return, SubsidioCausado: «Es el subsidio causado conforme al Decreto del subsidio para el empleo vigente.»; field TotalImpuestosRetenidos: «En caso de no existir la clave tipo deducción “002” (ISR), no se deberá registrar ningún dato en este campo.» (c) SAT, standard of the complement, revision C (29 Dec 2021, http://omawww.sat.gob.mx/tramitesyservicios/Paginas/documentos/Estandar_nomina12_29122021.pdf): OtroPago.Importe, «Si el valor del atributo TipoOtroPago es “002”, este atributo debe ser menor o igual que el valor del atributo SubsidioCausado.»; TotalImpuestosRetenidos, «Si no existen deducciones con clave “002”, este atributo no debe existir.» WHAT THE SOURCES DO NOT SAY, so never present it as quoted law: neither the decree nor the guide names the worker art. 96 spares from withholding. The decree grants the subsidy by income and makes no exception, and the annual computation subtracts what «le correspondió», so this system declares it as caused; that is its reading of the grant. Two more points are not verified: the guide's general text of OtroPago.Importe says «debe ser mayor que cero» while the standard allows 0.00 under key 002 (the guide derogated its Apéndice 5 «toda vez que se realizó el ajuste en la Revisión B del estándar»); and the standard caps SubsidioCausado at 407.02 for a receipt of up to 31 days, a figure older than the decree: a fortnight (264.30) is under it, a MONTHLY receipt of 2026 (535.65) is over it, and whether the PACs still apply that cap is not known here. When that worker also gets another taxed earning (the base is over the minimum of the period, in the same paycheck or in another run of the period) the policy `minimum_wage_isr_exemption` decides: `only_minimum_wage_paid` (default) applies the tariff to the whole base; `minimum_wage_part_never_taxed` applies it only to the excess over the minimum wage of the period. Paychecks already calculated are not changed: a run not yet approved takes the rule when it is calculated again. The worker's IMSS quota is still charged (LSS art. 36 is another task).
- A BENEFIT PAID IN A RUN CONSUMES ITS PROVISION (#111): the monthly close accrues aguinaldo, vacations and vacation premium to 6116 against 2202, 2203 and 2204 (`payroll accrue`, NIF D-3), so the entry of `pay-run post` does not expense them again. The policy `benefit_payment_against_provision` decides it. `consume_provision` (default): what the run pays in earning lines of type `aguinaldo`, `pto` or `vacaciones` (vacation days) and `prima_vacacional` is debited to its provision (by role: `provision_aguinaldo`, `provision_vacaciones`, `provision_prima_vacacional`) up to what the provision holds; only the excess is debited to wages (6110), in a line of its own (`… paid over its provision`), and what is not paid stays in the provision. THE MONTH IS THE UNIT: the accrual of a month (`payroll accrue`, the close) takes off, benefit by benefit and never below zero, what the pay runs dated in that month paid over the provision, so an aguinaldo paid on the 15th and accrued at the month's end is expensed once. It measures that from facts, never from the text of a line (the line's description is a label a reviewer may reword): what the runs paid in earning lines of the benefit, less what their entries debited to the provision's account, for runs whose entry is posted and not reversed. Its output names what it took off and what it did not (more than the month accrues; paid over a provision that already held the month, since the accrual never leaves a provision overdrawn; or not over the provision at all, when it still holds a balance the run did not consume). A run whose entry is still a draft in review is not counted and is named: the month accrues in full for it, so tell the human to settle the draft before the close, or to reverse the accrual and run it again after. What a provision holds for a run is the lesser of its posted balance at the end of the pay date's fiscal period and of everything posted, less what pay-run drafts awaiting review already debit to it, taken to the nearest cent (the accrual posts four decimals, the run's entry cents). `expense_on_payment` debits the whole gross to wages and is only for a firm that does not accrue: the entry is refused by key while the provision of a benefit the run pays carries a balance. Vacation days paid inside the `salary` line cannot be told from salary and stay expense: tell the human to itemize them as `pto` or `vacaciones`. An entity without those roles (they are seeded for Mexican entities) has nothing to consume. Entries posted before this rule are not corrected, and the finiquito endpoint only calculates: its amounts reach the ledger through the run that pays them.
- ISN (state payroll tax, employer's burden, roughly 1%–4%): computed per state from `mx_isn_tasas_estatales`, which is **empty on purpose**. If a state has no rate captured for the period, the calculation does NOT return zero: it names the state and the period that are missing. Say that out loud — a zero would be a filing omission dressed as a result. A human captures rates with `mnemosine isn rate set`, and that capture governs every firm on the installation.
- The regimes `escalonado` and `con_exencion` are refused, not approximated.
- IMSS employer quota: every rate of the `imss_employer` block is required. A missing block or rate does NOT yield zero: the calculation fails naming what is missing. `paychecks.taxable_wages_imss` holds the period's SBC base (capped at 25 UMA), not the ISR base.
- EMPLOYER CESANTÍA Y VEJEZ (#661): not one rate. Each contribution day pays the rate of its SBC band (LSS art. 168 fr. II a), read from the dated band table in `legal_parameters` (keys `imss.old_age.employer_rate.*`, one row per year from 2023 to 2030) with that day's capped SBC, UMA and minimum wage: 3.150 % up to one minimum wage, then by UMA band up to 7.513 % in 2026 and 8.603 % in 2027. The flat `imss_employer.cesantia_vejez` is not read. The policy `imss_old_age_band_edge` decides an SBC between two bands: `above_upper_edge` (default: above an upper edge by any amount pays the next band, so 352.00 in March 2026 pays 6.361 %) or `truncated_to_hundredths` (SBC ÷ minimum wage and SBC ÷ UMA cut to two decimals first: 6.026 %). «SM» is the general minimum wage of the area of the employer registration, the entity's declared zone (#196): 315.04 in the general zone and 440.87 at the northern border in 2026, so a border registration's SBC of 400.00 pays 3.150 % (189.00 a quincena) where the general zone pays 6.361 %. The policy `imss_old_age_minimum_wage_area` decides it: `employer_registration_area` (default) or `general_zone` (the 315.04 for every worker, as the SUA FAQ suggests to an employer registered in both zones). An entity that never declared its zone is measured with the general minimum, and the note says «general: the entity has not declared its zone»: give the `entity edit --minimum-wage-zone` command. A day without the table (before 2023) or without the minimum wage of its area loaded fails naming what is missing; it never falls back to 3.150 %. The employee's 1.125 % is unchanged. The calculation note names band, rate, UMA, minimum wage and rule.
- AGUINALDO (earning_type `aguinaldo`): exempt from ISR up to 30 daily UMA per calendar year (LISR art. 93 fr. XIV), with the UMA in force on the payment date; only the excess enters the ISR base. Each `paycheck_earnings` row stores `isr_exempt_amount` and `isr_taxable_amount`, and a second aguinaldo in the same year only gets what the first left of the cap. Rows written before this change have both NULL: they were taxed whole.
- VACATION PREMIUM (earning_type `prima_vacacional`): exempt from ISR up to 15 daily UMA per calendar year (same fraction), a cap of its own that the aguinaldo does not consume.
- OVERTIME (earning_type `overtime`, the double-paid hours): LISR art. 93 fr. I exempts 50 % of it, up to 5 daily UMA of the payment date per week of the period, within the LFT weekly limit, which is dated: 9 hours through 2027, then 10, 11 and 12 from 2030 (reform of DOF 01-05-2026). The weeks of a period are the policy `overtime_exempt_weeks`: `calendar_days_over_seven` (default: 586.55 a week, 1 256.89 a quincena from February 2026) or `whole_weeks_of_period` (a quincena is 2 weeks: 1 173.10). Under `exempt_by_law` EVERY `overtime` line must carry `hours` (a number, 0 or more): a line without them is refused, and so are hours that, added across the lines of the paycheck and the other runs (regular, off_cycle, correction, bonus, final) of the same pay period, pass the limit. The cap is also shared with those other runs. A run checks every employee's overtime before it writes the first paycheck. Triple-paid hours go as an earning of their own (for example `overtime_triple`) and are taxed whole. The policy `overtime_isr_exemption` decides: `exempt_by_law` (default) or `taxed_in_full` (no hours needed). A WORKER WHO EARNS THE MINIMUM WAGE OF THEIR ZONE (first sentence of fr. I, #196) has the overtime exempt IN FULL, with no 5 UMA cap: earning it means `annual_salary` / 365 at most the daily minimum, on the payment date, of the employee's `minimum_wage_zone`, else the entity's, else the general one (315.04 general, 440.87 northern border in 2026). The hours, the LFT limit and the triple-paid hours are as for everyone. A worker without `annual_salary`, one cent above the minimum, or in a zone whose minimum is not loaded gets the 50 %; a border entity that never declared its zone is compared with the general minimum, so give the `entity edit --minimum-wage-zone` command. - PAYROLL CFDI HEADER: `TipoNomina` is O for a regular run and E for a bonus, final or off-cycle run (a correction run is refused: its type depends on the run it corrects, which is not recorded); `PeriodicidadPago` comes from the pay schedule (02 weekly, 03 biweekly, 04 semimonthly/quincenal, 05 monthly) and is 99 when the run is E; `Antiguedad` is `P{n}W`, whole weeks from hire date to period end; a hire date after the period end, or under one week of seniority, is refused.
- PAYROLL CFDI: each `Percepcion` declares `ImporteGravado`/`ImporteExento` from the stored parts, and `TotalGravado`/`TotalExento` are their sums. A row with NULL parts is declared by its `is_taxable_isr` flag, which is how it was taxed.
- PAYROLL CFDI DEDUCTIONS AND SUBSIDY NODE (#92): the worker's IMSS is a `Deduccion` `TipoDeduccion="001"` (Seguridad social), the INFONAVIT credit discount a `Deduccion` `010` (Pago por crédito de vivienda) and the ISR retained a `Deduccion` `002`; an amount of zero writes no node. `TotalOtrasDeducciones` is the sum of the nodes whose key is not 002, `TotalImpuestosRetenidos` the sum of the 002 nodes (with no 002 node the attribute is not written: the standard forbids it), and `TotalDeducciones` (the voucher's `Descuento`) the sum of both. Every `TipoRegimen` 02 receipt carries `OtrosPagos` with `TipoOtroPago="002"` and its `SubsidioCausado`, also when the ISR is at or above the subsidy and when no subsidy was caused (`0.00` in both); `Importe` is only the cash handed over, so `Total = SubTotal − Descuento + TotalOtrosPagos`. A regime other than 02 with no subsidy carries no such node.
- PAYROLL CFDI DATES (#601): `FechaPago`, `FechaInicialPago`, `FechaFinalPago` and `FechaInicioRelLaboral` are the calendar days the pay period and the employee store, written `AAAA-MM-DD`; `Folio` is `NOM-<employee number>-<pay date>`. `Fecha` is the pay date at a FIXED 10:00:00: a literal, not the moment of issue nor the issuer's local time, so do not present it as the hour the CFDI was issued.
- A PAYCHECK IS STAMPED ONCE (#601): a paycheck whose payroll CFDI is `stamped` is refused with 409 before the PAC is called, and the message names its folio and when it was stamped; never tell the human to stamp it again to "refresh" it. A paycheck with no CFDI, or whose CFDI is `pending`, `failed` (a folio made by a simulated adapter) or `cancelled`, can be stamped; the system does not cancel a payroll CFDI yet. Two stamps of one paycheck at once reach the PAC once: the other waits and is refused with the folio, or gives up after the lock timeout saying a stamp is in progress. If the answer says the paycheck WAS stamped but the folio could not be written, the CFDI exists: hand that folio to a human and do not ask for another stamp.
