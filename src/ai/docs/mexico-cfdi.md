# Mexico: CFDI, multi-PAC stamping, and XML ingestion

## CFDI 4.0 essentials
- BOTH PUE AND PPD CREDIT VENDORS (AP), NEVER BANKS. Crediting banks directly would DOUBLE the outflow: the payment already shows up when the bank statement is reconciled, and the entry would book it a second time (`cfdi-taxonomy.ts`, `ingreso_recibido_pue.notes`). NOR DOES THE VAT SEPARATE THEM (MNE-001-492): both debit `iva_pendiente_acreditar`, because received VAT is credited when it is PAID (LIVA art. 5-III) and PUE is the issuer's label, not proof of payment (RMF rule 2.7.1.39); recording it as creditable inflates the month's crediting and is an audit risk. The later vendor payment generates its own entry, which releases the VAT to `iva_acreditable` (under PPD, with its payment complement).
- IVA IS ON A CASH BASIS (LIVA art. 1-B, art. 5-III) and WHICH account it hits depends on the side and, issued, on MetodoPago:
  - PUE received → DR 1135 "IVA Pendiente de Acreditar", as a PPD: the vendor payment releases it to 1130, and the approval of a draft against vendors refuses its VAT on 1130, naming `iva_pendiente_acreditar` (MNE-001-492). PUE issued → CR 2120 "IVA Trasladado".
  - PPD received → DR 1135 "IVA Pendiente de Acreditar" — NOT creditable yet; it PARKS there and the PAYMENT (with its REP) releases it to 1130, pro-rata to the amount paid. PPD issued mirrors this: parks in 2125 "IVA Trasladado No Cobrado", released to 2120 on collection — pro-rata to the cash PLUS what the customer withheld (`receipt apply --withholding`), since the withheld VAT was collected too: the customer remits it for the entity.
  - IEPS ON AN ISSUED SALE FOLLOWS THE SAME CLOCK (LIEPS art. 10: caused when collected and on what is collected). A PUE sale credits its IEPS to 2180 "IEPS por Pagar"; a PPD sale parks it in 2181 "IEPS Trasladado No Cobrado" (role `ieps_trasladado_no_cobrado`, Anexo 24 grouping code 209.02), and each collection — `receipt record`, its issued REP, `receipt apply`, a cheque's cashing — moves its share to 2180 on the same entry that moves the VAT from 2125 to 2120. IEPS is never revenue and never part of `tax_amount`. The approval refuses a PPD's IEPS in 2180 or in revenue, naming the IEPS figure. An entity seeded before the role refuses a PPD with IEPS until `npm run backfill:account-roles -- --aplicar` gives it 2181 and the role. Received IEPS is another matter (`ieps_acreditable`, #694).
  - NO MetodoPago ON THE CFDI: an issued one is booked as PUE, VAT in 2120 — a stamped CFDI 4.0 always carries the attribute (Anexo 20), so its absence is a defect of the XML, and PUE never recognises the VAT later than PPD would (late VAT bears updating and surcharges, CFF art. 21); the approval refuses that VAT in 2125, because no collection would release it. A received one stays PPD, VAT parked in 1135: input VAT is creditable only once paid (LIVA art. 5-III).
  - Never send PPD tax straight to 1130/2120: that claims/causes tax before the law does. The engine (iva-cash-basis.ts) picks the role from MetodoPago and the payment path does the release — your draft must use the parked account for PPD.
  - WHICH ENTITIES ARE ON CASH BASIS: the ones that keep Mexican books — incorporated in MX (the country is compared upper-cased and space-trimmed, so `mx` counts, and an unset or blank one counts as MX too: "ante la duda, mexicana"), or any entity whose accounting standard is `mx_nif` even if incorporated abroad. Since J0.1 that is the ONE predicate the chart seeder, the IVA engine and `doctor` share (`src/services/jurisdiction/`, `keepsMexicanBooks` / `sqlKeepsMexicanBooks`): an entity that was seeded with the four IVA accounts is exactly the one that parks VAT. An entity that DECLARES a country other than MX and does not keep its books in `mx_nif` never parks it — J0.1 did not change that: the engine never asks its MetodoPago and posts the tax to the generic `iva_acreditable` / `iva_trasladado` roles, which in a non-Mexican chart point at 1136 "Impuesto Acreditable sobre Compras" and 2135 "Impuesto sobre Ventas por Pagar" — a tax account, NOT an expense line. THE INBOX ASKS THE ENTITY TOO (MNE-001-742): on such an entity `bill inbox run` (and `POST /v1/pre-registrations/:id/process`) posts the VAT of a received CFDI, PPD or PUE, to `iva_acreditable`, as `bill create` and the received credit note do, and your own draft of one puts it there as well; the PUE and PPD lines above describe Mexican books. Until that task the inbox debited `iva_pendiente_acreditar` there, and since no payment releases VAT on those books it never left; on a neutral chart, which has no such role, the CFDI waited with "Missing accounts for roles: iva_pendiente_acreditar". Nothing corrects a bill posted that way and no posted entry is edited: its VAT shows on the trial balance in the pending account until a person reclassifies it with an entry of their own (DR the `iva_acreditable` account, CR the `iva_pendiente_acreditar` one). `cfdi post` ASKS THE ENTITY TOO (MNE-001-759): on such an entity a sale, PPD or PUE, credits its VAT to `iva_trasladado` and its IEPS to `ieps_por_pagar`, as `invoice create` does, because no collection moves tax on those books; the approval of a draft of a sale there counts the VAT on `iva_trasladado` and `iva_trasladado_no_cobrado` together and the IEPS on `ieps_por_pagar` and `ieps_trasladado_no_cobrado` together, and your own draft puts each in the first of the pair. Until that task `cfdi post` credited a PPD's VAT to `iva_trasladado_no_cobrado` and its IEPS to `ieps_trasladado_no_cobrado` there, and the collection never moved them; on a neutral chart the sale waited with "Missing accounts for roles: iva_trasladado_no_cobrado". Nothing corrects an invoice posted that way and no posted entry is edited: a person reclassifies it with an entry of their own (DR the `iva_trasladado_no_cobrado` account, CR the `iva_trasladado` one; for IEPS, DR the `ieps_trasladado_no_cobrado` account, CR the `ieps_por_pagar` one). A neutral chart has no `ieps_por_pagar` role, so a sale with IEPS still waits there. An entity id that returns no row (unknown, or another tenant's) is NOT treated as Mexican: no row, no cash basis.
- Foreign-currency PPD (R4): the parked VAT of a USD bill converts at the DOCUMENT's rate, and each partial payment releases q4(paid_so_far × rate) − q4(previously_released) — the telescoping sum reproduces EXACTLY what was parked, so the parked account returns to zero on the last payment instead of drifting by a rounding cent. That telescoping applies to the PENDING side only. The side that becomes due or creditable (2120 / 1130) is the tax figure of THAT payment, converted at the payment day's rate (LIVA arts. 1-B, 5-III and 11; art. 20 CFF), with its dollars in the FX columns. The gap between the two sides, like the payment's own realized difference, goes to 4320/6320.
- A RECEIVED CREDIT NOTE (type E, TipoRelacion 01 or 03) lowers the one bill it names, and its VAT leaves where that bill's VAT is: out of 1135 up to what the bill still has parked there, which is what its payments and the VAT of their early-payment discounts (MNE-001-597) have not taken out, and the rest out of 1130, because that share was already credited (LIVA arts. 5-III and 7; the rule lives in `received-credit-note.ts`). Never credit the whole VAT of a note to 1130 when its bill is an unpaid PPD. THE IVA PAPER SUBTRACTS ONLY THE 1130 SHARE, IN THE NOTE'S MONTH (MNE-001-760): `filing workpaper generate --form iva` takes off the creditable side what the note's own entry credited to 1130, split at the rates the note's CFDI states, as a negative line traced to that entry, so `IVA-WP-CREDITABLE-VS-LEDGER` stays silent; the share out of 1135 was never credited and is not subtracted. A note whose entry is reversed counts in no month: the tie-out of each month its entry or its mirror moved 1130 names it, and its own month the amended return (CFF art. 32). The DIOT does not change: a note is not a payment, and the batch file does not fill the §3.2 returns fields yet. The payment that settles a bill a note lowered releases all the bill still has parked (a rounding remainder would otherwise stay in 1135); with a discount or a write-off it keeps its pro rata, and the discount's or the write-off's own share of VAT leaves 1135 in its entry without becoming creditable. The door that posts it is `bill inbox run` (and `POST /v1/pre-registrations/:id/process`), run by a person: a note that does not fit its bill waits in the inbox with its reason. The upload posts one by itself only when a firm rule matched that both sets `processing_mode` to `auto` and requires the type, with a condition `document_type equals credit_note` among its `all` conditions; a rule with no conditions, or one that reaches the type through `in`, `regex` or `any`, never posts a note, and a later rule that sets `manual` or `hold` wins. The agent does not draft a received credit note: `ingest` sends no such CFDI to the model and points to `bill inbox run`.
- **PAYMENT BY CHEQUE IS EFFECTIVE WHEN THE CHEQUE IS CASHED, NOT WHEN IT IS HANDED OVER.** So the creditable VAT of a cheque payment belongs to the month the BANK PAID IT, not the month the cheque was written. A vendor payment with `--method check` in the functional currency holds its VAT in 1135: its own entry credits none, and `mnemosine bank check reconcile` posts the reclassification (1135 → 1130) dated on the clearing day, or on the later day the cheque's cash on account was applied to the bill, which is the month the DIOT counts it in (MNE-001-778). The IVA workpaper and the DIOT count that cheque in the month of its cashing, and in no month while no cashing is recorded (notice `DIOT-CHEQUE-NOT-CASHED`). If a user asks why a January cheque's VAT is not in January's return, this is why — and if it IS in January, its own entry credited it on the payment day (a cheque recorded before this rule, or one in a foreign currency): once cashed in a later month, January's workpaper says so with `DIOT-CHEQUE-CREDITED-BEFORE-CASHING` and that the filed return may need an amended one. THE SAME RULE ON THE CUSTOMER SIDE (MNE-001-556): a customer's cheque in the functional currency holds its charged VAT in 2125 when it is received and applied, and `mnemosine bank check reconcile --receipt <ref>` moves it to 2120 dated the day of the bank credit that cashed it, or, for an invoice its cash on account was applied to after that credit, on the day of that application, in an entry of its own (MNE-001-794): the IVA workpaper counts it there; the REP's FechaPago stays the day the cheque was received.
- Withholdings (ISR/VAT) subtract from the total. What the entity withholds as payer goes to the accounts the panel key `withholding_accounts_layout` chooses (roles `isr_retenido_por_pagar` and `iva_retenido_por_pagar`): `per_tax` (default) 2141 «ISR Retenido por Enterar» and 2142 «IVA Retenido por Enterar»; `single` both on 2143; `by_concept` ISR on leases to 2144 (grouping code 216.03, the role's `lease` qualifier), any other ISR withheld to 2145 (216.04) and VAT to 2142 (216.10). Payroll ISR stays on 2140 in every layout. A new entity is born with the layout; an existing one whose roles do not follow it (seeded before MNE-001-056: both on 2140) is governed by `withholding_accounts_existing`: `warn` (default) names it in `doctor` and in the close checklist (`withholding-accounts-layout`, never blocking), `repoint` repoints it when either key is set, `keep` leaves it. The fix is `mnemosine account role sync` (`--dry-run` first): it creates the missing accounts and repoints the roles, audited, posts no entry and leaves the balance already on 2140 where it is; moving that balance is a reclassification the accountant decides. A mapping set by hand (`account role set`) is never moved by it: it is listed as kept. When a legal entity (12-character RFC) receives an individual's lease CFDI (regime 606) or professional-fees CFDI (regime 612 declaring an ISR withholding), the classifier computes the withholding from `legal_parameters` on the CFDI date: 10 % ISR (LISR 106, 116) and two thirds of the transferred VAT (RLIVA 3-I). If the CFDI declares other amounts, the law's are booked and the entry is held for the accountant instead of posting; never rewrite the CFDI's figures to make it post.
- FREIGHT AND RESICO (MNE-001-057): a legal entity receiving land freight of goods (every concept in c_ClaveProdServ 781018) withholds 4 % of the consideration as VAT, whoever the carrier is (LIVA 1-A II c, RLIVA 3-II), only on the concepts that carry 16 % VAT and never more than the VAT charged: zero-rated international freight (LIVA 29-V) has nothing to withhold. Freight at the 8 % border rate is NOT computed (no legal_parameters row sets its withholding): its declared figures post with a warning to verify them. Freight next to any other concept (a good, loading, insurance) is not taken for freight and posts as declared. A legal entity paying an individual in RESICO (regime 626) withholds 1.25 % of ISR on the amount without VAT (LISR 113-J), plus two thirds of the VAT only when every concept is a professional service or a real-estate lease; what the CFDI declares is checked, never taken as proof, so a RESICO sale of goods that declares VAT withheld is a discrepancy. Under `withholding_accounts_layout=by_concept` a RESICO real-estate lease's ISR goes to the lease account (2144) and any other RESICO ISR to 2145. Both rates live in `legal_parameters` (migration 175). A CFDI whose declared withholding differs from the law's beyond rounding follows the panel key `withholding_mismatch` (a 612 fee declaring no ISR follows `fees_without_withholding` instead): `request_substitute_cfdi` (the default; CFF 26-I, LISR 27-V) holds it, proposes nothing and says to ask the vendor for a substitute; `withhold_by_law` proposes the law's figures and holds the entry for review; `record_as_issued` posts it as declared with a warning, and the close checklist lists it under `fees-without-withholding`. While the key is unanswered the CFDI is held and `mnemosine cfdi explain <uuid>` shows the blocking question `withholding_mismatch` with those three options. The CFDI is a third party's: never rewrite its figures.
- PROFESSIONAL FEES WITH NO ISR WITHHELD (MNE-001-148): a regime 612 CFDI to a legal entity whose concepts are ALL professional services and that declares no ISR withheld follows the policy `fees_without_withholding`. `request_substitute_cfdi` (the default) holds it in the inbox, proposes nothing and writes nothing to the ledger: the user asks the vendor for a substitute CFDI that declares the withholding. `withhold_by_law` proposes the entry with the law's withholding and holds it for review. `record_as_issued` posts it as declared, warns that the expense may not be deductible (LISR 27-V), and the close checklist lists it under `fees-without-withholding` (`closing explain fees-without-withholding` names the CFDIs). WHAT COUNTS AS FEES is read from the c_ClaveProdServ of every concept, conservatively (`withholding-law.ts`): consulting (8010), legal (8012), engineering (8110), software development (811115, 811116), accounting and audit (8411, except the advance key 84111506) and medical practice (8512). A purchase of goods, or a CFDI with any concept outside those families, is never taken for fees and posts as declared.
- Identifiers: UUID (fiscal stamp), series/folio, issuer/receiver RFC. The UUID is the system's dedupe key.

## What the API refuses before trying (G4a)
- Every REST route that mutates declares its risk class in its FIRST handler, the same four words the CLI uses (`lectura` / `escritura` / `irreversible` / `externo`), and the app REFUSES TO START if one does not — the same way the binary refuses to start on an impossible declaration. It also refuses if a declaration sits behind the handler: it would certify without protecting.
- Routes declared `irreversible` or `externo` REQUIRE an `Idempotency-Key` header. Same key + same payload replays the recorded response; same key + a DIFFERENT payload is a 409, never a silent second act. Stamping twice at a PAC does not undo either, which is why `externo` counts.
- Declared limits, so a body cannot become a denial of service: 1,000 lines per document, 5,000 movements per import, 500 applications per payment. Over the limit the rejection names the limit.
- `POST /v1/xml/upload` is declared `irreversible`, not `escritura`: with auto-posting it reaches the ledger. Its CLI twin (`mnemosine ingest`) always declared it that way, and the same act with two classes depending on the door was the whole point of G4a.

## Multi-PAC stamping (issued invoices)
- Failover chain with per-provider circuit breaker and per-tenant preferences. FOUR adapters are registered (G0 put them all through `integrationRegistry.register()` from one list — before, Sovos was in the router's dictionary only, so configuring it through the API died). THREE are SIMULATED (Finkok, SW Sapien, Edicom): they fabricate UUID and seal with `crypto.randomBytes`, and an anti-simulation lock guarantees a simulated folio is NEVER persisted as 'stamped' (it lands as 'failed' with a note). SOVOS is NOT simulated: it has a real SOAP implementation, injectable transport, and it THROWS rather than inventing a folio — but it has no credentials configured, so it cannot stamp either. `GET /v1/admin/integrations` now publishes the `simulado` flag per provider, so the difference is visible instead of assumed. Stamping is de facto off until a real PAC is contracted (§5 decision). Every adapter now carries a `datasheet` (provider, SAT authorization, sandbox URL, and `timbradoPresellado: true` as a literal type: an adapter that does not declare it does not compile — the CSD private key never leaves our vault). The default order for a tenant with no saved preferences is SW Sapien, Prodigia, Solución Factible, Finkok; Prodigia and Solución Factible have no adapter yet and the router skips ids it does not know. A partial `PUT .../pac/preferences/all` keeps the stored slots it does not mention, never persists an id with no adapter, and the route rejects such ids.
- ENTITY BOUNDARY (TEN-10): both /cfdi/stamp and /cfdi/cancel resolve the invoice WITHIN the caller's active entity. An invoice id belonging to a SIBLING company of the same tenant answers 404 — the same body a nonexistent id gets, so the 404 is not an existence oracle. This matters more here than elsewhere: stamping is external and irreversible, so the check runs BEFORE the PAC call, and the UPDATE that writes `cfdi_uuid` carries the entity too. Before this, stamping a sibling's invoice also OVERWROTE its `cfdi_uuid`, and that column held the folio needed to cancel it — the previous CFDI was left orphaned and uncancellable from the system. RLS does not cover this axis: it scopes by tenant, and two companies of one firm share it.
- AN INVOICE IS STAMPED ONCE (#696): an invoice whose CFDI is `stamped` is refused with 409 before the PAC is called, and the message names its folio, its status and when it was stamped; never tell the human to stamp it again to "refresh" it. An invoice with no CFDI, or whose CFDI is `pending`, `failed` (a folio made by a simulated adapter) or `cancelled`, can be stamped, the same rule as the payroll CFDI. Two stamps of one invoice at once reach the PAC once: the other waits and is refused with the folio, or gives up after the lock timeout saying a stamp is in progress. If the answer says the invoice WAS stamped but the folio could not be written, the CFDI exists: hand that folio and its PAC to a human and do not ask for another stamp.
- Human: POST /v1/invoices/:id/cfdi/stamp. CANCELLATION IS WITHDRAWN: /cfdi/cancel answers 501 — the human cancels at the PAC/SAT portal and reverses the entry with `mnemosine entry reverse`. Never tell a user the system cancels CFDIs.
- The XML the stamp route sends is the full CFDI 4.0 ingreso (Emisor, Receptor, Conceptos, Impuestos) built from the invoice and checked against the SAT's official XSD vendored in `src/services/sat/cfdi40/xsd`. It is built FAIL-CLOSED: a missing issuer regime or postal code, receiver regime/postal code/UsoCFDI, or line SAT product/unit code is a 422 that lists every gap. It carries NO Sello, NoCertificado or Certificado yet (sealing with the CSD is a separate task), so a PAC that requires a sealed document still rejects it. Payment is declared PPD/99 (paid later, «por definir»).

## Ingestion of received CFDIs (expenses)
3-layer pipeline (`mnemosine ingest *.xml` command):
1. Deterministic registration: CFDI 4.0/3.3 parsing, validation (UUID, RFCs, totals), dedupe by UUID/hash in xml_documents, vendor match by RFC, and firm rules (processing_rules) — if a rule auto-processes (e.g. small amount), it generates bill+journal entry without AI.
   - Totals include the ImpuestosLocales complement (a hotel's ISH). A received one posts it on a line of its own, on the `impuestos_locales_gasto` role account; the classifier's proposal for an issued one credits it to `impuestos_locales_por_pagar` (2190).
   - AN UNKNOWN ISSUER STOPS EVERYTHING AUTOMATIC. Registering a vendor creates a counterparty from data a third party wrote inside the XML, so no unattended path may do it: rules, scheduled batches, your ingestion and webhooks all refuse and leave the CFDI in the inbox saying which vendor it would have created. Only a human re-running it interactively (`bill inbox run --allow-new-vendor`, or `allow_new_vendor` in the REST body) authorizes the creation. Never tell a user you can add the vendor yourself — tell them to run `mnemosine vendor create --tax-id <RFC>` and re-run, which matches by RFC.
   - THE DIRECTION COMES FIRST, from the entity's RFC. A CFDI the entity ISSUED (issuer RFC = the entity's) is its sale, never its expense: it is registered, it is not matched as a vendor, the firm rules never see it, and neither `bill inbox run` nor the approval of a draft will ever turn it into a vendor bill. You see it with "Direction: issued" and draft the SALE: DR 1120 (or banks for a collected PUE) · CR revenue · CR 2120 for PUE, 2125 for PPD, and its IEPS, if any, CR 2180 for PUE, 2181 for PPD. Approving that draft creates the customer invoice (AR) for the receiver's RFC, who must already be a customer (otherwise the approval is refused with the `customer create` command). Under a generic RFC (XAXX010101000, XEXX010101000) the customer is the one the CFDI NAMES (case and extra spaces ignored, accents not), and two active customers that match, under any RFC, refuse the approval naming both numbers: one is archived first, never picked for the user. The entry must match the XML figure by figure, with a PPD's VAT in 2125 on Mexican books. Its issued REP then links deterministically and settles the invoice, moving the VAT from 2125 to 2120. A PERSON can also post it with no model and no draft: `mnemosine cfdi post <uuid>` (IA ✗: you cannot invoke it) writes the customer invoice with the CFDI's UUID and its entry in one act: DR 1120 · CR revenue · CR 2125 for PPD, 2120 for PUE or no MetodoPago, and IEPS on 2181 for PPD, 2180 otherwise; off Mexican books, the VAT on `iva_trasladado` and the IEPS on 2180 whatever the method (see above). Revenue goes to 4100 unless a line coded with `bill inbox edit <id> --line`, `--account <code>` or the stored default names another revenue account; the customer must already exist; `--dry-run` shows the invoice and the entry and writes nothing. An issued CFDI stored as a vendor bill before the direction existed was turned into a sale by migration 334: an account on its lines or as its default that is not revenue was cleared, so the line takes revenue unless someone codes it, and the account it held stays on the line as a reference that `cfdi post` prints beside the entry and never posts. A CFDI where the entity is NEITHER issuer nor receiver is refused before anything is written, naming both RFCs. Your summary and `ingest --dry-run` both state the direction.
2. YOU classify the rest: you receive the CFDI summary and create the draft (expense + VAT against VENDORS — never banks — with the VAT in 1135 `iva_pendiente_acreditar`, PUE or PPD, which the vendor payment releases to 1130; MNE-001-493). Check precedents and previous journal entries from the issuer first, and then READ THE FIRM'S POLICY PANEL with `get_accounting_policies` BEFORE deciding the treatment. The panel carries the criteria this system cannot decide on its own — capitalization threshold (expense vs fixed asset), restaurant meals, inventories, FX rate source, REP tolerances — plus the account role map that says which concrete account plays `activo_fijo`, `cxp`, `banco` or `iva_acreditable`. Use the answered ones and the account a role already names; invent neither.
   - The SYSTEM links your draft to this CFDI's pre-registration (you never write that link). When a human approves it, the vendor bill is born in the same transaction (header from the XML, `cfdi_uuid`, balance), the pre-registration closes and the entry posts with source_type='bill'. So your draft MUST match the CFDI figure by figure: total (credit to the `cxp` account, a PUE included: its payment is booked by its own entry), transferred VAT, each withholding in its role account, and subtotal minus discount as the sum of the non-tax debits. A difference above `cfdi_tolerancia_cuadre` (one cent by default) makes the approval fail naming the figure — never balance by moving cents to another account. The bill lines follow `lineas_factura_desde` (`poliza`: your non-tax debits; `conceptos_cfdi`: the CFDI concepts, refused unless your split matches them one to one). An issuer missing from the vendor catalog also refuses the approval, naming the RFC, the name and `mnemosine vendor create '<name>' --tax-id <RFC>`. With `proveedor_desconocido_al_aprobar = preguntar`, `mnemosine review` at a terminal asks the reviewer whether to register that vendor; only their yes creates it, together with the bill and the entry. Every unattended approval (threshold auto-post, approval policy, REST) refuses as with `rechazar`: never tell a user an approval policy or you can register the vendor.
   - A policy that comes back `unanswered` is NOT the firm's criterion: its `value` is only the system's stopgap. When such a policy is what decides THIS entry — two admissible answers would produce DIFFERENT entries for this CFDI — STOP, ask with `ask_user` citing the key, and create no draft. Applying the default there would post the system's stopgap as if the firm had decided it. If every admissible answer yields the same entry, the policy does not block you and you proceed without asking.
3. Auto-post gates (off by default). Two classes, and the difference matters:
   - INTEGRITY (no policy ever skips these): suspicious third-party text, more than one draft for one CFDI, currency ≠ functional, and your draft must balance against the CFDI total.
   - DISCRETIONAL: confidence ≥ minimum, amount ≤ cap (hard-clamped by a floor), vendor with a strong match. When one of these falls short, a standing approval policy granted by a human may still authorize it — the posting is then attributed to `policy:<id>`.
   Turning auto-post ON is the FIRM's decision, taken in the panel (`mnemosine pending`), and it costs evidence: days of shadow mode with human-decided verdicts agreeing. A local config file or a `--auto-post` flag can only be MORE strict — they can turn it off, never on. In shadow mode nothing posts: every gate runs and the verdict is recorded.

WITHOUT A MODEL (no provider configured, or one that refuses the credential), `mnemosine ingest` still runs layer 1 — rules and the vendor's default accounts — and leaves the rest in the inbox, saying how many were left to code; it no longer fails file by file. A credential helper (`api_key_cmd`) that fails is not «no model»: ingest stops at start naming the profile and its exit code, and a helper renewal that fails mid-batch leaves the rest of the batch to code instead of asking the model again. A CFDI already registered whose processing failed (model down, no key, a rule or posting error) is reprocessed with `mnemosine ingest <files> --retry`: without the flag it reports «duplicate». The retry refuses a CFDI that already has a bill, an entry, or a draft bound to it pending review or approved. A CFDI left to code is coded by a person with `mnemosine bill inbox edit <id> --line <n> --account <code>` (or `--account` alone as the default of every line), and only `bill inbox run` then posts it; the agent cannot invoke either. A CFDI the entity ISSUED that was left to code never goes through `bill inbox run`: `ingest` names `mnemosine cfdi post <uuid>` for it, and `mnemosine cfdi list --direction emitido --type cfdi_ingreso --status pending` lists the ones still to post. A vendor that keeps arriving is coded once as a firm rule: `mnemosine bill rule create --name <text> --when "emisor_rfc equals <RFC>" --then set_account=<code> --then set_processing_mode=auto` makes the next ingest post that vendor's CFDI with no model, and `mnemosine bill rule list` shows the rules in evaluation order and how often each fired. The pre-registration keeps which rule decided (`rules_applied`, `account_mapping_method = 'rule'`). The agent can list rules but cannot create them: a rule is the firm's criterion. CFDI that arrive in a SAT bulk-download package (`mnemosine sat package download <id> --import --live`, which only a person runs) enter by this same ingestion with `import_source = 'sat_download'`, deduplicated by UUID, and never consult the model: what the rules do not decide stays in the inbox. The agent can read the local mirror of those requests (`sat download status|list`, `sat quota show`), never call the SAT.

## Manual pre-registration management (human)
- CLI: `mnemosine bill inbox list` shows the queue and flags rows whose issuer is not in the vendor catalog; `bill inbox run [id]` processes one or, with `--bulk --query`, many.
- REST /v1: GET/PATCH /pre-registrations, POST /:id/process | /reject | /approve, POST /pre-registrations/bulk.
- Rules: GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /processing-rules (from the terminal: `bill rule create` and `bill rule list`; editing and deleting stay REST-only). Batches: /processing-batches (+ /:id/execute, /progress, /cancel).
