# Accounts receivable (customers and issued invoices)

## Customers
- Data: customer_number (C-...), company_name/first_name, tax_id (RFC/EIN), payment terms (default Net 30), credit limit and credit status (approved/on_hold/suspended), default income and AR accounts.
- The CFDI 4.0 fiscal profile lives apart from the commercial data: RFC, tax regime, postal code and UsoCFDI are set with `customer tax set`, validated against the SAT catalogs BEFORE they are written — a malformed RFC or an invented regime is refused at capture instead of failing at stamping weeks later. `customer edit` reaches neither the fiscal profile nor credit.
- Archiving is not deleting, and it is refused while the customer still owes something.
- YOU: search_customers, plus the read-only `customer list|show` and `customer tax show|list`. Human: POST/PATCH /v1/customers, `customer create|edit|archive|restore`, `customer tax set` — every write here is IA ✗.

## Invoices
- States: draft → pending → sent → viewed → partially_paid → paid; also overdue, void, cancelled, uncollectible.
- Of those, the only ones anything WRITES are draft (create), sent (issue, and again when cash is unapplied or a collection is reversed), partially_paid, paid and void. Nothing sets pending, viewed, overdue, uncollectible or cancelled: they are legal values of the column with no writer. So "viewed" does not mean the customer opened it — there is no read receipt and no email transport in this system — though aging does count a row that carries it.
- amount_due is maintained with each payment applied and each credit note applied, and back UP when cash is unapplied or a collection is reversed; aging uses amount_due > 0 and states sent/viewed/partially_paid/overdue.
- CFDI (Mexico): cfdi_status pending → stamped (or cancelled/failed); stamping goes through multi-PAC (see the mexico-cfdi doc).
- Automatic entry when an invoice posts: debit AR for the total; credit income per line; credit output VAT — 2120 IVA Trasladado for a PUE invoice, 2125 IVA Trasladado No Cobrado for a PPD one, released to 2120 by the collection (mexico-cfdi doc).
- ISSUING IS NOT SENDING. Posting that entry is what `invoice issue` does, and it neither stamps nor delivers; the delivery marks (sent_at, sent_to) are written only by POST /:id/send, which also posts. Nothing is transmitted either way. Never tell a human the invoice was emailed.
- A FOREIGN-CURRENCY INVOICE POSTS CONVERTED (R4, NIF B-15). If currency_code is not the entity's functional currency, issuing converts every line at the rate the `fuente_tipo_cambio` source (DOF by default) published for the invoice date — USD 1,000 at 17.50 posts 17,500 of revenue — and each line keeps its dollars in the FX columns; the rate is written back to invoices.exchange_rate. No published rate for that date: issuing FAILS CLOSED and names the command to load it (`fx rate set` / `fx rate download`). A rate already on the invoice that disagrees with the source: FX_RATE_CONFLICT, nothing posts. ITS COLLECTION CONVERTS TOO (MNE-001-082): the cash is converted at the `fuente_tipo_cambio` rate of the collection day (or an explicit rate), AR is credited per invoice at the rate the invoice was born with, and the gap is the REALISED exchange difference — gain to 4320 utilidad_cambiaria, loss to 6320 perdida_cambiaria. USD 1,000 invoiced at 17.50 and collected at 18.00 realises 500 of gain. The PPD IVA leaves 2125 at the invoice's rate (the one it was parked at) and is caused in 2120 at the collection day's rate, because the tax is caused when the money arrives (LIVA arts. 1-B and 11) and art. 20 CFF converts it at that day's rate; the gap is part of the realised difference (USD 580 of a PPD invoice at 17.50 collected at 18.00: 2125 −1,400, 2120 +1,440, gain 250). Four things still REFUSE: cash left on account in the foreign currency (FX_AR_ON_ACCOUNT_NOT_WIRED — apply the whole receipt to invoices in that currency), unapplying a foreign-currency collection, collecting a foreign invoice still carrying the 1.0 capture rate (it was never converted), and depositing it into a bank account kept in another currency (FX_BANK_CURRENCY_MISMATCH: the bank converted at its own rate). A CREDIT NOTE in a foreign currency REFUSES to issue (FX_CREDIT_NOTE_NOT_WIRED): it is not converted at birth yet, and posting it at face would unbalance AR; nothing posts. A foreign-currency REP creates its collection only when the `rep_moneda_extranjera` policy is `payment_day_rate` (default `no_casar`: review).
- Only a DRAFT is edited or deleted (`invoice edit|delete`; deleting demands a reason and leaves its folio as a DOCUMENTED gap — folios are never recycled, and `invoice series check` reports every gap, explained or not; a gap is explained only by a delete of the SAME legal entity, since an audit row of an entity-scoped fact records its entity; a row older than migration 324 names none and is attributed through its customer). An issued invoice is corrected by voiding it or with a credit note, never in place.
- Voiding annuls the ledger entry with a linked reversal (NIF B-1) and always requires a reason. `invoice void` REFUSES an invoice carrying a CFDI (cancel it before the SAT first) or with cash applied to it (unapply first); the REST route opts out of both guards to keep the contract it has always had.
- An invoice can also be born WITH THE OPENING BALANCE: `opening-balance import --subledger` turns each open document of the receivable control account into an invoice in status sent, linked to the opening entry, with its open balance as subtotal and no tax split. The load stops if the `cxc` role points at another account (`account role set cxc <code>` first), if a document is a credit balance or in another currency, or if its folio names an existing invoice. It is collected like any other. It can NOT be voided, on any surface, because voiding would reverse the whole opening it shares: it is cancelled with a credit note.
- Credit notes are their own document with their own folio (CN), not a negative invoice: draft → issue (posts DR devolucion_ventas 4400 + DR the VAT role / CR AR) → apply to invoices, which posts nothing more because the ledger already moved at issue. A NOTE OF TYPE `anticipo` APPLIES A CUSTOMER ADVANCE, AND IS NOT A RETURN (MNE-001-515): it posts DR anticipo_clientes 2150 + DR the VAT role / CR AR, so the advance is cancelled and revenue keeps the whole sale — an advance invoiced at 1,000.00 + 160.00 and applied to a sale of 5,000.00 + 800.00 leaves 2150 at 0, 4100 at 5,000.00 and the invoice owing 4,640.00. `credit-note issue` REFUSES it when its base is more than the customer's advance invoices still hold in 2150 (the credit of their own entries, less the `anticipo` notes already issued), and nothing is written: with no advance behind it the entry would lower the VAT due. Cash on account is not an invoiced advance, it is applied with `receipt apply`; an advance booked by a journal entry or carried in the opening balance is applied by a journal entry. The other three types (devolucion, descuento, correccion) post to 4400. An issued, unapplied note IS the customer's credit balance. A note tied to an invoice applies only to THAT invoice: the tie is what decides which VAT account it unwinds. A note created without a date is dated on today's calendar day in the zone of the `zona_horaria` policy (the entity's row, else the firm's, else America/Mexico_City) — never the UTC day, which at 20:00 in Mexico City is already tomorrow.
- Payment received: debit banks for the cash. The credit splits by what it settles — AR for what is APPLIED to invoices, and `anticipo_clientes` (2150) for cash received without naming one, because unapplied cash is an advance, not a settled receivable. For each PPD invoice it applies to, the same entry moves the parked VAT 2125 → 2120 for the collected share. Applying an advance later (`receipt apply`) is DR 2150 / CR AR plus that same VAT release, and moves no cash. `receipt apply` REFUSES an invoice of another customer than the receipt's (#664): the refusal names the invoice and both customers, and nothing is written; tell the user to record the collection under the invoice's customer. AN ADVANCE RECORDED WITH ITS IVA TREATMENT CAUSES ITS IVA WHEN COLLECTED (MNE-001-458, LIVA art. 1-B; Mexican books only): a receipt with no invoice whose treatment was stated (16, 8, 0, exempt or not-subject) credits 2150 with its BASE and 2120 with its IVA — 1,160.00 at 16 % is DR bank 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2120 160.00; the base is the cash over 1 + rate, rounded half up to the cent, and the IVA the difference — and at 0 %, exempt or not-subject the whole amount goes to 2150 with no IVA line. The receipt keeps the treatment, the rate, the base and the IVA (customer_payments.advance_*); revenue is not touched. There is no default rate: the rate of a sale that has not happened is a fact the accountant states. Cash on account with no treatment still goes whole to 2150, carrying its IVA. FROM THE TERMINAL (MNE-001-421) it is `receipt record --customer "Grupo Alameda" --amount 1160.00 --tax-rate 16`, with no invoice: `--tax-rate` takes 16, 8, 0, exempt (or exento) and not-subject (or no-objeto), is REQUIRED on an entity that keeps Mexican books and refused together with an invoice; the dry run, the confirmation and `--json` (advance_base, advance_tax_rate, advance_iva) show the three figures, and `receipt show` lists them. A receipt that is ALL advance owes the advance CFDI the client issues (Anexo 20, Apéndice 6), not a REP: `receipt show` says so on its REP line and `receipt list --needs-rep` leaves it out. If the customer has an open invoice whose own entry credited 2150 (an advance CFDI already in the books), the leaf WARNS, naming it, and still records: that advance's cash is recorded against that invoice, and a second advance for another sale is legitimate. That advance is for a sale NOT YET INVOICED: cash for an invoice already issued, the net of a customer that withheld included, is recorded against its invoice, not as an advance with `--tax-rate` (the withheld net's route is below). Two limits today: `receipt apply` REFUSES a receipt that carries one (ADVANCE_APPLICATION_NOT_WIRED) until applying cancels that IVA instead of causing it twice (MNE-001-422); and an advance received by CHEQUE is refused (ADVANCE_BY_CHEQUE_NOT_WIRED), because a cheque's IVA is caused the day the bank cashes it (LIVA art. 1-B, second paragraph) and recording that cashing does not exist yet (#700). `receipt reverse` mirrors the whole entry, the IVA included. A CUSTOMER THAT WITHHELD (MNE-001-113: a legal entity paying an individual's fees or lease pays net of 10 % ISR and two thirds of the VAT): `receipt apply --invoice INV:9533.33 --withholding isr:1000 --withholding iva:1066.67` settles the whole invoice — DR 2150 for the cash, DR 1145 `isr_retenido_a_favor` and DR 1146 `iva_retenido_a_favor` for what was withheld, CR AR for the sum — and the PPD VAT is released on the sum. The withholding is stored on the application, so `receipt unapply` and `receipt reverse` reopen it exactly. `--withholding` ONLY APPLIES TO AN INVOICE WHOSE RECEIVABLE WAS BOOKED GROSS (created with `invoice create` and issued): an invoice born from an issued CFDI that declares retenciones already debited 1145/1146 at issuance and its receivable is the net, so a withholding on it is refused — apply only the cash, or the same ISR and VAT would be credited twice against the provisional payment and the VAT return. It is also refused when the cash is zero (a withholding rides on a cash application), when the cash plus withholding exceeds what the invoice owes, when the VAT withheld exceeds the invoice's VAT, or the ISR its subtotal. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` show the withholding per invoice; `invoice show` and `receipt show` list it next to the cash of each allocation. `receipt record` does not take a withholding, so THE WITHHELD NET GOES BY ITS INVOICE IN THREE STEPS (MNE-001-459): `receipt record INV --amount 9533.33` for the cash, `receipt unapply PMT --invoice INV --reason …` to put it back on account, and `receipt apply PMT --invoice INV:9533.33 --withholding isr:1000 --withholding iva:1066.67` to close the invoice with the cash and what was withheld. On fees of 10,000.00 + 1,600.00: a PUE invoice's unapply is DR AR / CR 2150 9,533.33 with no IVA line, and the application DR 2150 9,533.33, DR 1145 1,000.00, DR 1146 1,066.67 / CR AR 11,600.00; a PPD invoice's record releases 1,314.9421, the unapply re-parks exactly that and the application releases 1,600.0000. Either way 2120 ends at the invoice's 1,600.00 and 2125 at 0. The unapply and the application are dated the day they are run; they take no `--date` yet (MNE-001-460). AN ADVANCE IN ANOTHER CURRENCY IS REFUSED (T23), for the same reason a collection of a foreign-currency invoice is: with no document to take the currency from it came from the customer's own `currency_code`, and nothing compared it with the entity's functional one — a 1,000 USD advance against an MXN entity was booked as 1,000 pesos and the entry balanced. The refusal names both currencies and says the rate and its SOURCE are the firm's decision (`fuente_tipo_cambio`), not a default. Tell the user to apply the receipt to a document in its own currency, or to record the advance in the functional one. A REVERSED RECEIPT KEEPS WHAT HAPPENED TO THE MONEY (MNE-001-509): `not_collected` when it never became the firm's, `taken_back` when it arrived and was taken away, as in a chargeback. The receipt keeps the cause with the instant it was stated (`receipt show --json` returns `reversal_cause` and `reversal_cause_stated_at`), and the mirrors and the closed allocations name it: «Reversed (not collected): …», «Reversed (taken back): …» or «Reversed (cause not stated): …». `receipt reverse --cause not_collected|taken_back` states it; any other value is a usage error and nothing is written. With no `--cause` a cheque takes `not_collected`, because it is paid the day the bank cashes it (LIVA art. 1-B), and every other method is reversed with none. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` (`cause`, `cause_defaulted`, `warnings`) say which happened, and warn in two cases: `not_collected` on a card, which is paid when it is accepted, and no cause on Mexican books, where the warning states the rule: the IVA stays in the month it was collected until a cause is stated with `receipt cause record`. A RECEIPT REVERSED WITH NO CAUSE GETS ONE AFTERWARDS (MNE-001-511): `receipt cause record <receipt> --cause not_collected|taken_back --reason "…"` writes it once, with the instant of the statement (later than `reversed_at`, which is how a later statement is told apart), and leaves it in the audit trail with its reason. It posts nothing and rewords nothing: the mirrors and the closed allocations keep «Reversed (cause not stated): …». It is refused, and nothing is written, when the receipt is not reversed or already has a cause: a stated cause cannot be corrected. The cause moves no figure of the IVA paper yet, which still leaves a reversed receipt out of every month.
- `receipt unapply` RE-PARKS ONLY WHAT WAS PARKED (MNE-001-517): an application that stored the IVA it released moves exactly that back, 2120 → 2125. EVERY NEW APPLICATION STORES IT, 0 INCLUDED, ON ANY ENTITY (MNE-001-459), whether `receipt record` or `receipt apply` wrote it, so a stored 0 re-parks nothing and is no estimate. Only a row written before that, which stored none, gets a pro-rata estimate, said on the entry's lines and in the command's output, only when the invoice is PPD and the entity keeps Mexican books — a PUE invoice, or an entity that does not keep them, re-parks nothing, and the unapply moves only the cash and the withholding.

## What YOU do
- get_aged_receivables (aging with days_overdue; negative = not yet due), search_customers, and query the generated journal entries (search_journal_entries with entry_type auto_invoice/auto_payment — credit notes post as auto_invoice, applications and unapplications as auto_payment).
- A receipt's journal entry carries the receipt's own date (#211). Before that fix, west of UTC the entry landed one day before `customer_payments.payment_date`, and a receipt dated January 1st was refused. If older data shows a receipt and its entry a day apart, that is the cause. THE READING SIDE IS FIXED TOO (#241): a DATE column comes back from pg as a Date at LOCAL midnight, and `.toISOString()` re-read it in UTC, so EAST of Greenwich a stored day was reported as the previous one — including the day that decides whether an early-payment discount is still open. Dates you are shown now come through the calendar reader or as text from SQL; never re-derive one with `toISOString`.
- get_aged_receivables ages the DUE DATE at as_of_date but reads the balance AS IT STANDS TODAY: it does not reconstruct what was owed on a past date. Fine for a collections call, wrong for an auditor — say which one you are giving.
- Without a date, "today" in the aging, in `invoice list` (days overdue, --overdue-days), in `customer list` (what is past due) and in `customer show` is the calendar day in the zone of `zona_horaria`, as for credit notes — so an invoice due today is not overdue at 20:00 in Mexico City.
- `customer show --as-of` and `invoice list --as-of` DO reconstruct the past: both read one shared amount-due-as-of that subtracts payments (cash plus any withholding applied with them) dated on or before the date (a reversed one only until its reversal date, an allocation only until it was unapplied) and credit notes APPLIED on or before it — dated by when the note was applied, not issued, because that is the only date an application carries. `invoice list --as-of` leaves out draft, void and cancelled invoices, as `customer show` does.
- `mnemosine ar reconcile` (read-only, you may run it) squares the subledger — open invoices minus unapplied credit notes — against the cxc control account, and NAMES the manual entries posted straight to the control with no document behind them, which no aging report can see (the engine's own reversal of an invoice or collection entry is not one of them). A foreign-currency invoice weighs its book value (amount due × the rate it was posted at; `foreign_open` lists each currency), and so does an unapplied foreign-currency credit note (at its invoice's rate; `foreign_unapplied`), and the closing FX revaluation and its day-1 mirror are the `fx_revaluation` line of the subledger, never manual entries. `ar check` runs the named battery (subledger-delta, negative-balance, over-application, orphan-application, duplicate-invoice, stale-unapplied-cash, missing-uuid, cancelled-cfdi-open, stamped-without-entry); each `count` is the full total, not the length of the five-row sample. Both are TODAY, never as-of.

## Human (REST /v1/invoices)
- POST / (create), POST /:id/send, POST /:id/payments (record a collection), POST /:id/void (requires reason), POST /:id/cfdi/stamp, POST /:id/cfdi/cancel.
- POST /:id/send is the issuing call: it posts the revenue and AR entry and marks the invoice sent. It transmits nothing and answers `transmitted: false`; the sending is the user's own.
- CLI: `invoice create|issue|void|edit|delete`, `credit-note create|issue|apply`, `receipt record|apply|unapply|reverse` and `receipt cause record` (collections). Those writes are IA ✗ — you may read and propose, never run them; `invoice list|show|series`, `credit-note list|show`, `receipt list|show` and the `ar` commands are yours. Stamping has no CLI: it is the REST route only.
