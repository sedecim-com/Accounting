# Accounts receivable (customers and issued invoices)

## Customers
- Data: customer_number (C-...), company_name/first_name, tax_id (RFC/EIN), payment terms (default Net 30), credit limit and credit status (approved/on_hold/suspended), default income and AR accounts.
- The CFDI 4.0 fiscal profile lives apart from the commercial data: RFC, tax regime, postal code and UsoCFDI are set with `customer tax set`, validated against the SAT catalogs BEFORE they are written — a malformed RFC or an invented regime is refused at capture instead of failing at stamping weeks later. `customer edit` reaches neither the fiscal profile nor credit.
- Archiving is not deleting, and it is refused while the customer still owes something.
- YOU: search_customers, plus the read-only `customer list|show` and `customer tax show|list`. Human: POST/PATCH /v1/customers, `customer create|edit|archive|restore`, `customer tax set` — every write here is IA ✗.

## Invoices
- States: draft → pending → sent → viewed → partially_paid → paid; also overdue, void, cancelled, uncollectible.
- Of those, the only ones anything WRITES are draft (create), sent (issue, and again when cash is unapplied or a collection is reversed), partially_paid, paid and void. Nothing sets pending, viewed, overdue, uncollectible or cancelled: they are legal values of the column with no writer. So "viewed" does not mean the customer opened it — there is no read receipt and no email transport in this system — though aging does count a row that carries it.
- amount_due is maintained with each payment applied and each credit note applied, and back UP when cash is unapplied or a collection is reversed; aging uses amount_due > 0 and states sent/viewed/partially_paid/overdue.
- CFDI (Mexico): cfdi_status pending → stamped (or cancelled/failed); stamping goes through multi-PAC (see the mexico-cfdi doc).
- Automatic entry when an invoice posts: debit AR for the total; credit income per line; credit output VAT — 2120 IVA Trasladado for a PUE invoice, 2125 IVA Trasladado No Cobrado for a PPD one, released to 2120 by the collection (mexico-cfdi doc).
- ISSUING IS NOT SENDING. Posting that entry is what `invoice issue` does, and it neither stamps nor delivers; the delivery marks (sent_at, sent_to) are written only by POST /:id/send, which also posts. Nothing is transmitted either way. Never tell a human the invoice was emailed.
- A FOREIGN-CURRENCY INVOICE POSTS CONVERTED (R4, NIF B-15). If currency_code is not the entity's functional currency, issuing converts every line at the rate the `fuente_tipo_cambio` source (DOF by default) published for the invoice date — USD 1,000 at 17.50 posts 17,500 of revenue — and each line keeps its dollars in the FX columns; the rate is written back to invoices.exchange_rate. No published rate for that date: issuing FAILS CLOSED and names the command to load it (`fx rate set` / `fx rate download`). A rate already on the invoice that disagrees with the source: FX_RATE_CONFLICT, nothing posts. ITS COLLECTION CONVERTS TOO (MNE-001-082): the cash is converted at the `fuente_tipo_cambio` rate of the collection day (or an explicit rate), AR is credited per invoice at the rate the invoice was born with, and the gap is the REALISED exchange difference — gain to 4320 utilidad_cambiaria, loss to 6320 perdida_cambiaria. USD 1,000 invoiced at 17.50 and collected at 18.00 realises 500 of gain. The PPD IVA leaves 2125 at the invoice's rate (the one it was parked at) and is caused in 2120 at the collection day's rate, because the tax is caused when the money arrives (LIVA arts. 1-B and 11) and art. 20 CFF converts it at that day's rate; the gap is part of the realised difference (USD 580 of a PPD invoice at 17.50 collected at 18.00: 2125 −1,400, 2120 +1,440, gain 250). Four things still REFUSE: cash left on account in the foreign currency (FX_AR_ON_ACCOUNT_NOT_WIRED — apply the whole receipt to invoices in that currency), unapplying a foreign-currency collection, collecting a foreign invoice still carrying the 1.0 capture rate (it was never converted), and depositing it into a bank account kept in another currency (FX_BANK_CURRENCY_MISMATCH: the bank converted at its own rate). A CREDIT NOTE in a foreign currency REFUSES to issue (FX_CREDIT_NOTE_NOT_WIRED): it is not converted at birth yet, and posting it at face would unbalance AR; nothing posts. A foreign-currency REP creates its collection only when the `rep_moneda_extranjera` policy is `payment_day_rate` (default `no_casar`: review).
- Only a DRAFT is edited or deleted (`invoice edit|delete`; deleting demands a reason and leaves its folio as a DOCUMENTED gap — folios are never recycled, and `invoice series check` reports every gap, explained or not; a gap is explained only by a delete of the SAME legal entity, since an audit row of an entity-scoped fact records its entity; a row older than migration 324 names none and is attributed through its customer). An issued invoice is corrected by voiding it or with a credit note, never in place.
- Voiding annuls the ledger entry with a linked reversal (NIF B-1) and always requires a reason. `invoice void` REFUSES an invoice carrying a CFDI (cancel it before the SAT first) or with cash applied to it (unapply first); the REST route opts out of both guards to keep the contract it has always had.
- An invoice can also be born WITH THE OPENING BALANCE: `opening-balance import --subledger` turns each open document of the receivable control account into an invoice in status sent, linked to the opening entry, with its open balance as subtotal and no tax split. The load stops if the `cxc` role points at another account (`account role set cxc <code>` first), if a document is a credit balance or in another currency, or if its folio names an existing invoice. It is collected like any other. It can NOT be voided, on any surface, because voiding would reverse the whole opening it shares: it is cancelled with a credit note.
- Credit notes are their own document with their own folio (CN), not a negative invoice: draft → issue (posts DR devolucion_ventas 4400 + DR the VAT role / CR AR) → apply to invoices, which posts nothing more because the ledger already moved at issue. A NOTE OF TYPE `anticipo` APPLIES A CUSTOMER ADVANCE, AND IS NOT A RETURN (MNE-001-515): it posts DR anticipo_clientes 2150 + DR the VAT role / CR AR, so the advance is cancelled and revenue keeps the whole sale — an advance invoiced at 1,000.00 + 160.00 and applied to a sale of 5,000.00 + 800.00 leaves 2150 at 0, 4100 at 5,000.00 and the invoice owing 4,640.00. `credit-note issue` REFUSES it when its base is more than the customer's advance invoices still hold in 2150 (the credit of their own entries, less the `anticipo` notes already issued), and nothing is written: with no advance behind it the entry would lower the VAT due. Cash on account is not an invoiced advance, it is applied with `receipt apply`; an advance booked by a journal entry or carried in the opening balance is applied by a journal entry. The other three types (devolucion, descuento, correccion) post to 4400. An issued, unapplied note IS the customer's credit balance. THE IVA PAPER SUBTRACTS AN ISSUED NOTE IN THE MONTH ITS ENTRY MOVES 2120 (MNE-001-565, LIVA art. 7): `filing workpaper generate --form iva` takes out the IVA the note's own entry debited to 2120, and its base split at its invoice's rates (a note with no invoice at the rate its figures state), as a negative line traced to that entry, so `IVA-WP-CHARGED-VS-LEDGER` stays silent — a PUE sale of 10,000.00 + 1,600.00 with a note of 1,000.00 + 160.00 in its month is 9,000.00 / 1,440.00. A NOTE ON A PPD INVOICE TAKES ITS IVA FROM WHERE THE INVOICE'S IVA SITS (MNE-001-575, LIVA arts. 1-B and 7): out of 2125 up to what the invoice still has parked there — what its collections have not released and its earlier notes have not taken — and the rest out of 2120, restituted, because that share was already caused; the received side's rule. On an uncollected invoice it all comes from 2125 and the paper takes nothing out (the later collection releases less); on one collected whole it all comes from 2120 and the paper takes it out in the note's month — a PPD sale of 1,000.00 + 160.00 collected whole, with a note of 100.00 + 16.00, is 900.00 / 144.00 and 2125 stays at 0. A note whose entry debited 2125 more than its PPD invoice still had parked that day (the IVA of what the invoice still owed) was posted before the posting split it: the excess is IVA already caused that stays in 2120, the paper subtracts nothing of it, and the notice `IVA-WP-CREDIT-NOTE-AFTER-COLLECTION` names the note, its invoice and that IVA; the entry stands (NIF B-1). An `anticipo` note is not a return, but its IVA leaves the month all the same, because the advance caused it when collected. A note whose entry was reversed counts in no month, and the tie-out of each month its entry or mirror moved names it and the amended return (CFF art. 32). A note tied to an invoice applies only to THAT invoice: the tie is what decides which VAT account it unwinds. A note created without a date is dated on today's calendar day in the zone of the `zona_horaria` policy (the entity's row, else the firm's, else America/Mexico_City) — never the UTC day, which at 20:00 in Mexico City is already tomorrow.
- Payment received: debit banks for the cash. The credit splits by what it settles — AR for what is APPLIED to invoices, and `anticipo_clientes` (2150) for cash received without naming one, because unapplied cash is an advance, not a settled receivable. For each PPD invoice it applies to, the same entry moves the parked VAT 2125 → 2120 for the collected share. THE PARKED IEPS MOVES WITH IT (#699, LIEPS art. 10): an invoice whose own entry parked IEPS in the `ieps_trasladado_no_cobrado` role (2181) has its share moved to 2180 `ieps_por_pagar` on that same entry, pro rata to what is collected and the last collection the exact remainder, by `receipt record`, its REP, `receipt apply` and a cheque's cashing alike; `receipt unapply` of a receipt with no treatment keeps it caused with the IVA (MNE-001-574, LIEPS art. 5o.-C, below), otherwise it gives back exactly what that receipt moved, and `receipt reverse` unwinds it. An issued PPD CFDI parks its IEPS there at approval, by either door (MNE-001-505); an entity seeded before the role refuses that CFDI, naming `npm run backfill:account-roles -- --aplicar`, which gives it 2181 and the role. Applying an advance later (`receipt apply`) is DR 2150 / CR AR plus that same VAT release, and moves no cash. A RECEIPT BY CHEQUE HOLDS ITS VAT UNTIL THE BANK CASHES IT (MNE-001-556, #700; LIVA art. 1-B, second paragraph): while a cheque in the functional currency has no cashing recorded, `receipt record --method check` posts DR bank / CR AR with no 2125 / 2120 line, `receipt apply` of its cash on account releases nothing, and each allocation stores 0. `mnemosine bank check reconcile --receipt PMT-… [--transaction <id>]` records the bank credit that cashed it: the receipt keeps that credit and its date, and DR 2125 / CR 2120 is posted for what its live allocations have not released, dated the credit's day, in that month; a closed month refuses it. A deposit of several cheques is one recording (MNE-001-557): repeat `--receipt` and name the credit with `--transaction`; the credit must be their exact sum, and each receipt gets its own entry. A credit another receipt holds, or that a live bank match ties to anything but those receipts or the bank lines of their own entries, is refused, naming the match; `bank match unapply` undoes a wrong one. Applied after the cashing, `receipt apply` releases as any other method. `receipt unapply` before the cashing re-parks nothing, and after it keeps caused what the cashing released (MNE-001-574, below); `receipt reverse` mirrors the cashing entry too. `receipt show` says whether the cheque is cashed. What tells a held cheque is its method and its cashing, not the stored 0, which every allocation that released nothing carries. A cheque in another currency still releases on its receipt day, and a cheque recorded before this rule keeps the release its own entry made. A cheque nobody cashes keeps its VAT in 2125: if a user asks why a cheque's VAT is not in 2120, ask whether its cashing was recorded. THE IVA PAPER COUNTS A CHEQUE IN THE MONTH OF ITS CASHING (MNE-001-507), as the ledger does: `filing workpaper generate --form iva` counts each event of a held cheque (its applications, unapplies, reversal and advance) on its own day or the cashing's, whichever is later, traced to the cashing entry, and in no month while no cashing is recorded; a cheque recorded before this rule, or in another currency, keeps its receipt day, as its entry did. A cheque unapplied before its cashing counts in the cashing's month the cash still on account, taxed that day, and none in the unapply's; a cheque reversed before any cashing counts and is named in no month. A MONTH THAT MAY HOLD AN UNRECORDED CASHING DOES NOT SETTLE: for each held cheque with no cashing, from its receipt's month, if the statement of its account (of every account in its currency when the receipt names none, such as a REP) covers the month from the receipt's day and holds no OPEN credit — at least the cheque's amount, held by no receipt as its cashing, and matched live to nothing but that receipt or its own entry's bank line — the paper warns (`IVA-WP-CHEQUE-NOT-CASHED`) with the cheque, its date and its IVA, and settles; otherwise `IVA-WP-CHEQUE-CASHING-UNRECORDED` blocks it, naming the open credits or the uncovered statement, and the month carries no settlement until the cashing is recorded, the credit is matched to what it paid, or the statement is imported. `receipt apply` REFUSES an invoice of another customer than the receipt's (#664): the refusal names the invoice and both customers, and nothing is written; tell the user to record the collection under the invoice's customer. AN ADVANCE RECORDED WITH ITS IVA TREATMENT CAUSES ITS IVA WHEN COLLECTED (MNE-001-458, LIVA art. 1-B; Mexican books only): a receipt with no invoice whose treatment was stated (16, 8, 0, exempt or not-subject) credits 2150 with its BASE and 2120 with its IVA — 1,160.00 at 16 % is DR bank 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2120 160.00; the base is the cash over 1 + rate, rounded half up to the cent, and the IVA the difference — and at 0 %, exempt or not-subject the whole amount goes to 2150 with no IVA line. The receipt keeps the treatment, the rate, the base and the IVA (customer_payments.advance_*); revenue is not touched. There is no default rate: the rate of a sale that has not happened is a fact the accountant states. Cash on account with no treatment, which only the service's other callers still record (the terminal states or derives one, below), goes whole to 2150, carrying its IVA. FROM THE TERMINAL (MNE-001-421) it is `receipt record --customer "Grupo Alameda" --amount 1160.00 --tax-rate 16`, with no invoice: `--tax-rate` takes 16, 8, 0, exempt (or exento) and not-subject (or no-objeto), is REQUIRED on an entity that keeps Mexican books when there is no invoice, and with an invoice goes only with `--on-account` (the excess, below); the dry run, the confirmation and `--json` (advance_base, advance_tax_rate, advance_iva) show the three figures, and `receipt show` lists them. A receipt that is ALL advance owes the advance CFDI the client issues (Anexo 20, Apéndice 6), not a REP: `receipt show` says so on its REP line and `receipt list --needs-rep` leaves it out. If the customer has an open invoice whose own entry credited 2150 (an advance CFDI already in the books), the leaf WARNS, naming it, and still records: that advance's cash is recorded against that invoice, and a second advance for another sale is legitimate. That advance is for a sale NOT YET INVOICED: cash for an invoice already issued, the net of a customer that withheld included, is recorded against its invoice, not as an advance with `--tax-rate` (the withheld net's route is below). A HELD CHEQUE'S ADVANCE IS NOT APPLIED AGAINST IVA NOBODY HAS CAUSED (MNE-001-572, #789; the owner's «Rechazar + clave para diferir» of 19 Nov 2026): while a cheque with a treatment is not cashed, `receipt apply` of cash that takes its advance's IVA to a PPD invoice, or to a PUE one at another treatment or several, is REFUSED with ADVANCE_BY_CHEQUE_NOT_CASHED, naming the receipt, the invoice, the IVA, `mnemosine bank check reconcile --receipt PMT-…` and the panel key `cheque_advance_before_cashing`, and nothing is written; record the cashing and apply it then. A PUE invoice at the receipt's own single treatment is applied before the cashing (its entry cancels the IVA the invoice caused in its month; the cashing causes the advance's on the bank's day), and so is an advance at 0 %, exempt or not-subject, or cash with no treatment. UNDER `defer_to_cashing` (MNE-001-573) the application to a PPD invoice goes through: its entry cancels nothing (2150 Dr 1,160.00 / 1120 Cr 1,160.00, `advance_iva_deferred` in `--json`), the allocation keeps the IVA it took, and the cashing nets it against the share the invoice releases that day (2125 Dr 160.00 / 2120 Cr 160.00, with no 2150 line; a partial 580.00 moves 80.00 from each); unapplied before the cashing it goes back whole to 2150 and the cashing causes the advance's IVA. A PUE invoice at another treatment releases no share to net against, so it is refused under either answer. Applied AFTER the cashing to a PUE invoice issued in an earlier month, the dry run, the confirmation, `--json` `warning` and the entry name that month and the IVA: it caused in its own month what art. 1-B puts on the cashing day, and neither the ledger nor the paper can move a month already passed. The IVA paper counts an application to a PUE invoice on its own day, as the ledger cancels it. AN ADVANCE RECEIVED BY CHEQUE CAUSES ITS IVA THE DAY THE BANK CASHES IT (MNE-001-558, #700; LIVA art. 1-B, second paragraph): `receipt record --method check --tax-rate 16` keeps the same base and IVA on the receipt, but its entry is DR bank 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,160.00 with no 2120 line, and the leaf says so; `bank check reconcile --receipt PMT-…` then posts DR 2150 160.00 / CR 2120 160.00 dated the bank credit's day, and its preview and `--json` (`advance_iva`) show that line. Until the cashing is recorded, 2150 holds the advance with its IVA. THE EXCESS OF A COLLECTION IS AN ADVANCE TOO (MNE-001-462; the owner's answers 2 and 3 of 16 Nov 2026 on #623): `receipt record INV --amount 1500.00 --on-account` on an invoice of 1,160.00 at 16 % posts DR bank 1,500.00 / CR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 293.10 / CR 2120 46.90 (340.00 / 1.16, half up), and the receipt keeps 16.00, 293.10 and 46.90. The rate is `--tax-rate` when stated (`--tax-rate 0` leaves CR 2150 340.00; `--tax-rate` with an invoice and no `--on-account` is a usage error), and otherwise the invoice's own, read by the same reader as the IVA paper (`sale-sources.ts`): one treatment is that one; several take the highest rate that carries IVA, or exempt when none does, the assumption that cannot understate the tax; an invoice only at 0 % or only exempt leaves the whole excess in 2150 with that treatment, and one born from a CFDI outside the object of IVA leaves it not-subject. The dry run, the confirmation, `--json` (`advance_tax_rate`, `advance_tax_rate_from`, `advance_tax_rate_assumed`) and, when the rate was taken from the invoice, the entry's description say the rate, its invoice and what was assumed. An entity off Mexican books posts the excess as before, whole to its customer advances. AN EXCESS RECEIVED BY CHEQUE KEEPS ITS SPLIT AND CAUSES ITS IVA ON THE CASHING DAY (MNE-001-572, #789): the receipt stores the treatment, base and IVA as above, its entry holds the excess whole in 2150 and the leaf says so, and `bank check reconcile --receipt` posts that IVA with the invoice's share: 1,500.00 on a PPD invoice of 1,160.00 cashed on 06-01 is 2125 Dr 160.00 / 2150 Dr 46.90 / 2120 Cr 206.90 that day. Unapplied before the cashing, its cash goes back whole to 2150 with no IVA line (`iva_at_cashing` in `--json`, and the leaf says it): the cashing causes that IVA too, 2150 Dr 206.90 / 2120 Cr 206.90 in the same example. After the cashing an unapply is as described below. There is no panel key: an overpayment the firm will give back is the same advance completed by a later event. LIVA art. 1-B, first paragraph: «se consideran efectivamente cobradas las contraprestaciones cuando se reciban en efectivo, en bienes o en servicios, aun cuando aquéllas correspondan a anticipos, depósitos o a cualquier otro concepto sin importar el nombre con el que se les designe». LIVA art. 7: the taxpayer that «devuelva los anticipos o los depósitos recibidos» deducts them from the value of its acts «en la siguiente o siguientes declaraciones de pago del mes de calendario que corresponda», «siempre que expresamente se haga constar que el impuesto al valor agregado que se hubiere trasladado se restituyó», with a document that states the consideration and the restituted IVA apart and identifies the original CFDI (second paragraph). Source: the LIVA the Cámara de Diputados publishes, last reform DOF 12-11-2021, read 16 Nov 2026. So the return of an overpayment is `receipt remit`, which will post the art. 7 restitution and does not exist yet (phase 2): until then nothing here takes that IVA back, and a refund is not recorded through receipts. ON A RECEIPT WITH A TREATMENT, THE CASH AN UNAPPLY PUTS BACK IS A TAXED ADVANCE (MNE-001-462, the owner's «no re-park» of 17 Nov 2026): the record-time cash of such a collection goes back on account as an advance at the receipt's own treatment, split as the advance is, so on a PUE invoice its unapply is DR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2120 160.00 (`advance_iva_restored` 160.00), and on a PPD one DR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2125 160.00 with no 2120 line (`iva_reparked` 0). All the cash on account of such a receipt is then one advance (1,500.00 with 206.90 of IVA in the example), and applying it cancels its IVA as any advance, by the same cumulative target: 1,160.00 applied to another invoice cancels 160.0027 and the last 340.00 cancel 46.8973, so 2150 ends at 0 and 2120 at the invoices' own IVA. A RECEIPT WITH NO TREATMENT KEEPS ITS IVA CAUSED TOO (MNE-001-574, #792; the owner's «Mantener causado» of 19 Nov 2026, #623): an unapply moves no money, and only a return, discount or bonus takes the IVA back (LIVA art. 7), so unapplied from a PPD invoice, a plain receipt's cash goes back to 2150 for its base and the share it released stays in 2120: 1,160.00 collected on a PPD invoice of 1,000.00 + 160.00 unapplies as DR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2125 160.00 with no 2120 line, `iva_reparked` 0.0000 and `iva_kept_caused` 160.0000 in `--json`, and the dry run, the confirmation and the leaf say «IVA kept caused 160.0000 (LIVA art. 1-B)». Each closed allocation stores what it kept (`unapply_kept_iva_amount`, migration 338): its release whole, or, for a row that took cash already back on account, its plain part's share (g × plain / amount, four decimals) while that cash's own IVA goes back with it. That cash is then an advance carrying its IVA: applying it again, to the same invoice or another, cancels that IVA against the share it releases by the same cumulative target, so the application is DR 2150 1,000.00 / DR 2125 160.00 / CR AR 1,160.00 with no 2120 line and each invoice's IVA is caused once. The IEPS that receipt moved stays in 2180 too, and the invoice's share goes back to 2181 because the invoice is owed again (MNE-001-590, LIEPS art. 10): a PPD invoice of 1,000.00 + IEPS 80.00 + IVA 172.80 collected and unapplied is DR AR 1,252.80 / CR 2150 1,000.00 / CR 2125 172.80 / CR 2181 80.00 with no 2180 line. The cash then carries that IEPS as it carries the IVA, read back from the receipt's tagged IEPS lines, and an application takes it at the cash's own ratio (e) against the share the invoice releases (r): DR 2181 r and CR 2180 r − e (a debit when negative, no line at zero), so each invoice's IEPS is caused once, and cash applied to an invoice with no IEPS lowers 2180 by the IEPS it carried, as the IVA's CR g − c does. A withholding's row keeps its whole stored release, which nets when the cash is applied again with the same withholding. A row written before 049 still re-parks its estimate, a PUE invoice and an entity off Mexican books keep nothing, a receipt with a treatment is as above, and a row unapplied before migration 338 re-parked and stays so; an unapply posted before MNE-001-590 put nothing back in 2181, so its cash carries no IEPS. THE PANEL KEY `plain_receipt_unapply_iva` (MNE-001-585) can re-park instead, only in the month of the collection: `keep_caused` is the default (LIVA arts. 1-B and 7); under `repark_same_month` each row whose IVA was released in the calendar month of the unapply (its `applied_on`, or the receipt's date, or a cheque's cashing day when its IVA waited for the bank) posts as before 574 — 1,160.00 collected on 05-15 and unapplied on 05-20 is DR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,160.00 / DR 2120 160.00 / CR 2125 160.00, `iva_reparked` 160.0000, `iva_kept_caused` 0.0000, no marker, and the IEPS goes back to 2181 with it — while a row released in an earlier month stays caused, because that month may already be declared (CFF art. 32), and the dry run says so; a cheque the bank has not cashed released nothing, so nothing is re-parked. The IVA paper does not follow the key. APPLYING AN ADVANCE TO THE INVOICE OF ITS SALE CANCELS THE IVA IT CAUSED, IT DOES NOT CAUSE IT TWICE (MNE-001-422): `receipt apply PMT --invoice INV:1160.00` against a PUE invoice is DR 2150 the base / DR 2120 the advance's IVA that cash carries / CR AR the cash, the effect of the TipoRelacion 07 egreso that cancels the advance — 1,160.00 at 16 % applied to a sale of 5,000.00 + 800.00 is DR 2150 1,000.00, DR 2120 160.00 / CR AR 1,160.00, so 2120 ends at the sale's 800.00 and 2150 at 0. The IVA a part cancels is the advance's IVA times the share of the advance consumed, a cumulative target minus what the receipt's live allocations already cancelled, so parts of 386.67, 386.67 and 386.66 cancel 53.3338, 53.3338 and 53.3324, which is 160.0000. An application takes first the receipt's cash on account that is no advance, then the advance (on a receipt with a treatment none of it is plain cash any more, MNE-001-462); an invoice at another rate, 0 % included, cancels the advance's IVA the same way and causes its own. Each allocation stores what it took (`advance_base_amount`, `advance_iva_amount`), so `receipt unapply` credits back exactly that (on a PUE invoice CR 2150 the base, CR 2120 the IVA) and applying again cancels the same IVA; `receipt reverse` mirrors every entry. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` show `advance_iva_cancelled` per invoice (`advance_iva_restored` on unapply), and `receipt show` lists `advance_iva` per allocation. When the invoice is a PUE issued on or before the advance and the application falls in a later month, the advance's month carried that IVA twice in 2120 (the invoice's and the advance's): the dry run, the confirmation, `--json` (`warning`) and the entry say so, and the user reviews that month's return. AGAINST A PPD INVOICE (MNE-001-461) the share the cash settles still leaves 2125 as for any collection (`g`, the invoice's cumulative target), and the advance's IVA that cash carries (`c`) takes its place in 2120: DR 2150 the base (a − c), DR 1145/1146 any withholding, DR 2125 g / CR AR the cash plus withholding, CR 2120 g − c — a debit when negative and no line at zero, so the IVA of that cash is caused once. 1,160.00 at 16 % applied in full to a PPD sale of 5,000.00 + 800.00 is DR 2150 1,000.00, DR 2125 160.00 / CR AR 1,160.00 with no 2120 line; collecting the other 4,640.00 then moves 640.00 from 2125 to 2120, because the advance's allocation counts in the invoice's target, so 2120 ends at 800.00 and 2150 and 2125 at 0, in parts too. A PPD sale at 0 % releases nothing, so the application is the PUE one. The allocation stores g (`iva_reclass_amount`) and c. `receipt unapply` of it RE-PARKS NOTHING OUT OF 2120 (the owner's «no re-park» of 17 Nov 2026, #623; LIVA art. 1-B): the cash goes back on account as the advance it was, its IVA still caused, so the entry is DR AR / CR 2150 a − c, CR 2125 g (the invoice is owed again) and CR 2120 c − g, none when they are equal — the unapply's month ends the share and gets the same cash back as an advance received, netting 0 — and `iva_reparked` is 0; only when the invoice's rate is above the advance's does the excess of g over c leave 2120. THE IVA PAPER COUNTS EACH OF THESE EVENTS IN THE MONTH WHOSE 2120 IT MOVED (MNE-001-423): `filing workpaper generate --form iva` counts an advance's base and IVA in the month it was collected, in the box of its treatment (a not-subject one in the no-objeto base; exempt and not-subject ones enter the proportion of LIVA art. 5 fr. V); the month of an application takes out what the allocation stored (a PUE sale of 5,000.00 + 800.00 with that advance applied is 4,000.00 / 640.00 there, and a PPD one nets to 0, its share against the advance); and the month of an unapply adds the advance back and takes out the PPD share it had released. A month already shown does not move, and a PPD share counts on the day its cash was applied (`applied_on`), not its receipt's day, so `IVA-WP-CHARGED-VS-LEDGER` stays silent. ANY OTHER UNDO OF A COLLECTION FOLLOWS WHAT HAPPENED TO THE MONEY (MNE-001-518, #666; no panel key): an unapply moves no money, so the month the share was collected keeps it, and the unapply's month lists the ended share and the same cash back on account, already taxed (LIVA art. 1-B), netting 0; applying that cash later nets it out again where it is applied. On a receipt with a treatment (MNE-001-462) that cash is the advance it comes back as, so the paper counts it once, as the advance: the unapply's month ends the share and adds the advance back, netting 0, and the later application takes it out of the advance; the ledger re-parks nothing there, so `IVA-WP-CHARGED-VS-LEDGER` stays silent. A receipt with no treatment is the same since MNE-001-574: the ledger keeps the share caused and the later application takes the cash with the IVA it carries, exactly as the allocation stored it, so both months are silent. Only a row unapplied before migration 338, or one re-parked under `plain_receipt_unapply_iva = repark_same_month` (MNE-001-585), re-parked (DR 2120 / CR 2125): that month's `IVA-WP-CHARGED-VS-LEDGER` names the unapply as the cause of its gap, and the month of the later application names it too. A REVERSAL FOLLOWS ITS STATED CAUSE, NOT ITS METHOD (MNE-001-510, #701). `not_collected` counts in no month, because money that never arrived caused no IVA (art. 1-B; a cheque is paid when it is cashed, second paragraph): each month it counted in drops it and `IVA-WP-RECEIPT-REVERSED` names the reversal (date, mirror, base and IVA) and that a filed return is corrected by an amended one (complementaria, CFF art. 32), and the reversal's month names its mirror. `taken_back` takes no IVA out: the collection month keeps it, and the reversal's month says it leaves only by an art. 7 restitution (the document of its second paragraph, RLIVA art. 24): an issued credit note, which the paper subtracts in its own month (MNE-001-565). A reversal with NO CAUSE is kept where it was collected, and both months name it and the command that states one (`receipt cause record`); a cheque with no cause stored (reversed before migration 327) reads as `not_collected`. An advance follows its receipt's cause. An invoice that a kept reversal reopened can be collected again: that collection counts whole in its own month, which names the earlier reversal, whose IVA stays where it was with no restitution counted. A cause stated later moves the months it changes, and each of them names the statement and its day. A cheque recorded at the default `spei` and reversed with no cause is kept: its IVA may be overstated, never understated. This covers PROCEDURE A of Anexo 20, Apéndice 6 (the sale invoiced whole, the advance cancelled by an egreso 07). Under PROCEDURE B (only the remainder is invoiced) do not `receipt apply` the advance to the remainder invoice: move it to revenue with a journal entry (DR 2150 / CR revenue) until `advance apply --method` exists (MNE-001-525). `receipt reverse` mirrors the whole entry, the IVA included. A CUSTOMER THAT WITHHELD (MNE-001-113: a legal entity paying an individual's fees or lease pays net of 10 % ISR and two thirds of the VAT): `receipt apply --invoice INV:9533.33 --withholding isr:1000 --withholding iva:1066.67` settles the whole invoice — DR 2150 for the cash, DR 1145 `isr_retenido_a_favor` and DR 1146 `iva_retenido_a_favor` for what was withheld, CR AR for the sum — and the PPD VAT is released on the sum. The withholding is stored on the application, so `receipt unapply` and `receipt reverse` reopen it exactly. `--withholding` ONLY APPLIES TO AN INVOICE WHOSE RECEIVABLE WAS BOOKED GROSS (created with `invoice create` and issued): an invoice born from an issued CFDI that declares retenciones already debited 1145/1146 at issuance and its receivable is the net, so a withholding on it is refused — apply only the cash, or the same ISR and VAT would be credited twice against the provisional payment and the VAT return. It is also refused when the cash is zero (a withholding rides on a cash application), when the cash plus withholding exceeds what the invoice owes, when the VAT withheld exceeds the invoice's VAT, or the ISR its subtotal. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` show the withholding per invoice; `invoice show` and `receipt show` list it next to the cash of each allocation. `receipt record` does not take a withholding, so THE WITHHELD NET GOES BY ITS INVOICE IN THREE STEPS (MNE-001-459): `receipt record INV --amount 9533.33` for the cash, `receipt unapply PMT --invoice INV --reason …` to put it back on account, and `receipt apply PMT --invoice INV:9533.33 --withholding isr:1000 --withholding iva:1066.67` to close the invoice with the cash and what was withheld. On fees of 10,000.00 + 1,600.00: a PUE invoice's unapply is DR AR / CR 2150 9,533.33 with no IVA line, and the application DR 2150 9,533.33, DR 1145 1,000.00, DR 1146 1,066.67 / CR AR 11,600.00; a PPD invoice's record releases 1,314.9421, the unapply keeps it caused (DR AR 9,533.33 / CR 2150 8,218.3879 / CR 2125 1,314.9421) and the application releases 1,600.0000 net of it, CR 2120 285.0579. Either way 2120 ends at the invoice's 1,600.00 and 2125 at 0. AN APPLICATION AND AN UNAPPLY ARE DATED (MNE-001-460): `receipt apply --date` and `receipt unapply --date` (YYYY-MM-DD; default today in the zone of `zona_horaria`) date the entry, the allocation (`applied_on`) and the invoice's last payment, and an unapply also the closure of the allocation (`unapplied_at`), so the IVA and the IEPS the event moves belong to that day's month and a balance read "as of" a day sees the ledger and the invoice move together. Cash for an invoice already issued is applied dated on the day it arrived. Refused, writing nothing: a date after today, a date before the receipt's last posted entry (its record, an earlier application or unapply, or a cheque's cashing), an application dated before its invoice, and a date in a hard-closed or locked period. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` (`date`) show the day, and `receipt show` lists each allocation's `applied_on`. `receipt record` applies on the receipt's own date. AN ADVANCE IN ANOTHER CURRENCY IS REFUSED (T23), for the same reason a collection of a foreign-currency invoice is: with no document to take the currency from it came from the customer's own `currency_code`, and nothing compared it with the entity's functional one — a 1,000 USD advance against an MXN entity was booked as 1,000 pesos and the entry balanced. The refusal names both currencies and says the rate and its SOURCE are the firm's decision (`fuente_tipo_cambio`), not a default. Tell the user to apply the receipt to a document in its own currency, or to record the advance in the functional one. A REVERSED RECEIPT KEEPS WHAT HAPPENED TO THE MONEY (MNE-001-509): `not_collected` when it never became the firm's, `taken_back` when it arrived and was taken away, as in a chargeback. The receipt keeps the cause with the instant it was stated (`receipt show --json` returns `reversal_cause` and `reversal_cause_stated_at`), and the mirrors and the closed allocations name it: «Reversed (not collected): …», «Reversed (taken back): …» or «Reversed (cause not stated): …». `receipt reverse --cause not_collected|taken_back` states it; any other value is a usage error and nothing is written. With no `--cause` a cheque takes `not_collected`, because it is paid the day the bank cashes it (LIVA art. 1-B), and every other method is reversed with none. The dry run, the confirmation and `--json` (`cause`, `cause_defaulted`, `warnings`) say which happened, and warn in two cases: `not_collected` on a card, which is paid when it is accepted, and no cause on Mexican books, where the warning states the rule: the IVA stays in the month it was collected until a cause is stated with `receipt cause record`. A RECEIPT REVERSED WITH NO CAUSE GETS ONE AFTERWARDS (MNE-001-511): `receipt cause record <receipt> --cause not_collected|taken_back --reason "…"` writes it once, with the instant of the statement (later than `reversed_at`, which is how a later statement is told apart), and leaves it in the audit trail with its reason. It posts nothing and rewords nothing: the mirrors and the closed allocations keep «Reversed (cause not stated): …». It is refused, and nothing is written, when the receipt is not reversed or already has a cause: a stated cause cannot be corrected. The next IVA papers apply it (MNE-001-510, above) and name the statement as a later event.
- Payment trace for the Anexo 24 (#611, migration 335): in a receipt the money comes from the customer, so the póliza's `Transferencia` declares the CUSTOMER's bank (`BancoOriNal`) and account (`CtaOri`, when known) as the origin and our bank account (`--bank`: its SAT bank code and number) as the destination. `receipt record --payer-bank <c_Banco> [--payer-account <number>]` captures them on the receipt, or `--payer-foreign-bank <name>` for a foreign bank, which the voucher declares as the c_Banco code `999` with the bank's full name in `BancoOriExt` (on a cheque, `BanEmisExt`); a bare `999` with no name is a valid node with a `banco-en-catalogo` warning on its voucher (MNE-001-401). A RECEIPT BY CHEQUE declares a `Cheque` whose issuer is the customer's bank and whose `CtaOri` is the account it is drawn on, never our bank or account: `receipt record --method check --check-number <n> --payer-bank <c_Banco> --payer-account <number>`, with or without `--bank`; a number over the node's 20 characters is refused. A code off the SAT's c_Banco list, a national and a foreign bank together, an account with no bank, or payer data on a cash or card receipt is refused before anything is written. A receipt recorded without them is named by `e-accounting voucher generate`, and the finding names the command that completes it; `receipt record` and `receipt trace record` repeat that finding on stderr once they write (MNE-001-571), nothing under `--json`. A RECEIPT ALREADY REGISTERED IS COMPLETED, NEVER RECORDED AGAIN (MNE-001-570, #612): `receipt trace record <receipt> --payer-bank <c_Banco> [--payer-account <number>] --reason "<evidence>"` (or `--payer-foreign-bank`, `--check-number`, and `--bank "<account>"` for our bank account) fills only EMPTY fields of a `completed` or `reversed` receipt from its evidence (the CEP, the deposit slip, the statement), a receipt a REP created included, under the same rules as `payment trace record`: the same value again is unchanged, a different one is refused naming the stored one, the payer checks above run on the merged trace, and `--bank` is accepted only when the ledger account of that bank account is on a line of the receipt's entry and its currency is the receipt's. It posts nothing, so it runs in any period state, and leaves one `audit_log` row (`complete_trace`) under `customer_payments`. Never tell the user to reverse and record again: that leaves the original entry and its mirror untraced.
- `receipt unapply` RE-PARKS ONLY WHAT WAS PARKED (MNE-001-517), and since MNE-001-574 a stored release is not re-parked at all unless `plain_receipt_unapply_iva` says `repark_same_month` (above): what it released goes back to 2125 because the invoice is owed again, against the IVA the cash keeps caused back on account (see the receipt with no treatment above), as for an application that took an advance's IVA (MNE-001-461) or any application of a receipt with a treatment (MNE-001-462), and leaves 2120 only by what exceeds it. EVERY NEW APPLICATION STORES IT, 0 INCLUDED, ON ANY ENTITY (MNE-001-459), whether `receipt record` or `receipt apply` wrote it, so a stored 0 re-parks nothing and is no estimate. Only a row written before that, which stored none, gets a pro-rata estimate, said on the entry's lines and in the command's output, only when the invoice is PPD and the entity keeps Mexican books — a PUE invoice, or an entity that does not keep them, re-parks nothing, and the unapply moves only the cash and the withholding. AFTER A CREDIT NOTE LINKED TO THE INVOICE (MNE-001-581), what goes back to 2125 is only what brings it to the IVA of the balance owed again (the total, less the cash and withholding still applied, less the notes; a cheque not yet cashed still counts as owed): with a PPD 1,000.00 + 160.00 collected whole and a note of 200.00 + 32.00 that took its IVA from 2120, 128.00 goes back to 2125 and the other 32.00 stays out of it, where the note restituted it (LIVA art. 7); the entry's IVA line, or its AR line when there is none, names it. A receipt with no treatment then keeps caused only those 128.00 (MNE-001-574, decision (A) on #792): the note's restitution stands, and the cash it covers is a balance owed back, not consideration, so the unapply is DR AR 1,160.00 / CR 2150 1,032.00 / CR 2125 128.00 with no 2120 line, and the row stores 128.0000. Applying that cash again takes its IVA at the pool's own ratio (128 over 1,160.00), so 928.00 applied to the invoice cancels 102.40 against the 128.00 it releases and 2120 rises by 25.60, which the 232.00 still on account carries until it is applied; a known effect, recorded as a follow-up. `receipt reverse` mirrors every entry as before and, after such a note, posts one entry of its own (`receipt_reversal_iva`, DR 2125 / CR 2120) for what its mirrors put back that no reopened balance carries. With no note, both are as before.
- A CUSTOMER'S CASH ON ACCOUNT CAN BE TRANSFERRED TO ANOTHER CUSTOMER, AS AGREED (MNE-001-498, #695 part 1/2; migration 339): `receipt transfer record <A's receipt> --customer <B> --amount <n> --date <agreed day> --reason "<the agreement>"` is a cession of A's credit (CCF arts. 2029 and 2038) that collects, returns and causes nothing. B gets a credit row of its own in customer_payments (`payment_method` `balance_transfer`, `transferred_from_payment_id` naming A's receipt, `transfer_reason`, no bank account, numbered TRF-YYYY-NNNNN) and the transfer posts its own entry on the agreed day: DR 2150 «A's credit assigned to B» / CR 2150 «B's credit from A», with no bank, revenue or IVA line (`source_type` `customer_credit_transfer`), one audit row with the reason, both customers and the amount, and no payment node in the Anexo 24 voucher. The dry run shows the entry and both balances before and after (A's receipt 1,160.00 → 0.00, B 0.00 → 1,160.00). A RECEIPT'S CASH ON ACCOUNT IS NOW ITS AMOUNT LESS ITS LIVE ALLOCATIONS LESS ITS LIVE TRANSFERS OUT, in `receipt apply`, `receipt show`, `receipt list --unapplied` and `ar check stale-unapplied-cash` alike, and a transfer is one of the receipt's events, so a later apply or unapply cannot be dated before it. Refused before anything is written, each by its key: more than the cash on account, the same customer, another entity's customer (404), a receipt not in the functional currency or not completed, a future date or one before the receipt's last posted event (a closed period is refused by posting), no `--reason`, and an entity off Mexican books. Until part 2 (MNE-001-499) it also refuses an advance with its IVA caused (any receipt with a treatment, a cheque's excess included, and cash an unapply put back keeping its IVA caused, MNE-001-574), a cheque the bank has not cashed, and a transfer row as the source; `receipt reverse` of A's receipt is refused while a transfer out of it is live, and applying, unapplying or reversing the transfer row is refused, so B's credit sits on account, visible, until part 2. A wrong transfer is corrected meanwhile with a transfer back, B → A. The bank match never offers a transfer row (it moved no money), and ISR-WP-ADVANCES does not count its credit to 2150 as an advance collected. It is IA ✗, like every receipt write.

## What YOU do
- get_aged_receivables (aging with days_overdue; negative = not yet due), search_customers, and query the generated journal entries (search_journal_entries with entry_type auto_invoice/auto_payment — credit notes post as auto_invoice, applications and unapplications as auto_payment).
- A receipt's journal entry carries the receipt's own date (#211). Before that fix, west of UTC the entry landed one day before `customer_payments.payment_date`, and a receipt dated January 1st was refused. If older data shows a receipt and its entry a day apart, that is the cause. THE READING SIDE IS FIXED TOO (#241): a DATE column comes back from pg as a Date at LOCAL midnight, and `.toISOString()` re-read it in UTC, so EAST of Greenwich a stored day was reported as the previous one — including the day that decides whether an early-payment discount is still open. Dates you are shown now come through the calendar reader or as text from SQL; never re-derive one with `toISOString`.
- get_aged_receivables ages the DUE DATE at as_of_date but reads the balance AS IT STANDS TODAY: it does not reconstruct what was owed on a past date. Fine for a collections call, wrong for an auditor — say which one you are giving.
- Without a date, "today" in the aging, in `invoice list` (days overdue, --overdue-days), in `customer list` (what is past due) and in `customer show` is the calendar day in the zone of `zona_horaria`, as for credit notes — so an invoice due today is not overdue at 20:00 in Mexico City.
- `customer show --as-of` and `invoice list --as-of` DO reconstruct the past: both read one shared amount-due-as-of that subtracts payments (cash plus any withholding applied with them) applied on or before the date — an allocation is dated by its own `applied_on`, and one written before migration 332 by its receipt's date (a reversed receipt counts only until its reversal date, an allocation only until it was unapplied) and credit notes APPLIED on or before it — dated by when the note was applied, not issued, because that is the only date an application carries. `invoice list --as-of` leaves out draft, void and cancelled invoices, as `customer show` does.
- `mnemosine ar reconcile` (read-only, you may run it) squares the subledger — open invoices minus unapplied credit notes — against the cxc control account, and NAMES the manual entries posted straight to the control with no document behind them, which no aging report can see (the engine's own reversal of an invoice or collection entry is not one of them). A foreign-currency invoice weighs its book value (amount due × the rate it was posted at; `foreign_open` lists each currency), and so does an unapplied foreign-currency credit note (at its invoice's rate; `foreign_unapplied`), and the closing FX revaluation and its day-1 mirror are the `fx_revaluation` line of the subledger, never manual entries. `ar check` runs the named battery (subledger-delta, negative-balance, over-application, orphan-application, cross-party-application, duplicate-invoice, stale-unapplied-cash, missing-uuid, cancelled-cfdi-open, stamped-without-entry); each `count` is the full total, not the length of the five-row sample. `cross-party-application` is a warning: it names live allocations whose invoice belongs to another customer than their receipt, written before `receipt apply` refused them; it blocks nothing. `orphan-application` blocks: it names the live allocations of a void, failed or reversed receipt, each with the receipt, its status, the invoice and the amount (MNE-001-589). Since MNE-001-584 a reversal refuses rather than leave one, so a row it finds is older or written by hand. Such a row still counts as collected, so the invoice's next collection releases none of its IVA; the reversal already reopened the invoice and mirrored its entries, so only the row's close is missing, and no command writes it. Tell the user to report it, and not to collect that invoice again until it is settled; never apply or unapply to repair it. Both are TODAY, never as-of.
