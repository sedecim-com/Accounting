# Month-end close — detailed checklist

Work top to bottom; every item is either verified clean or turned into a
draft (yours) or a named command (the human's).

## Cash and banks (1100s)
- [ ] Every bank account reconciled against the imported statement.
- [ ] Petty cash counted and the difference (if any) drafted.

## Receivables (1200s)
- [ ] Aging report reviewed; PPD invoices with collections have their
      payment complement recorded.
- [ ] Doubtful accounts estimate updated per NIF C-3 (draft + cite).

## Payables (2100s)
- [ ] Received invoices (bills) approved and scheduled.
- [ ] Accrued expenses without invoice provisioned (NIF C-9).

## Payroll (5100s / 2xxx)
- [ ] Payroll provision for the period drafted (ISR retained, IMSS, SUA).
- [ ] Payroll CFDI stamped — human action, cite the exact command from the
      payroll doc.

## Taxes
- [ ] IVA trasladado vs acreditable reconciled (see the diot-checklist
      skill for the DIOT side).
- [ ] Provisional ISR payment computed and provisioned.

## Fixed assets (1500s)
- [ ] Monthly depreciation drafted per NIF C-6, same method as the
      precedent.

## Final
- [ ] `mnemosine pending` empty.
- [ ] Trial balance signs reviewed.
- [ ] Human runs `mnemosine close`.
